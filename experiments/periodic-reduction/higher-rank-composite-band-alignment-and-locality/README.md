# Higher-rank composite-band alignment and locality

## Purpose

This publication-independent prospective family supplies the common analytic model for
the Paper 3 higher-rank study. It separates retained rank, noncommuting Givens-circuit
depth, admissible alignment family, and spatial dimension across orthogonal periodic
one-, two-, and three-dimensional controls.

The family has no executable composition, immutable version, retained run, or evidence
package. The first bounded 1-D P0/G0 development tranche now has selected parent
coefficients, an exact analytic exterior-isolation energy, real Givens-angle law,
rank/depth ladder, alignment
channels, and cross-rank normalizations. Its meshes, tolerances, supports, schemas,
verifier, and resource limits remain open. Dimension-specific experiment identities will
be assigned only after those controls are reviewed. Empty dimensional package
placeholders are intentionally not created.

## Current scientific boundary

The proposed parent has an isolated rank-$r$ subspace embedded in dimension $2r$, an
analytic global periodic frame, optional retained--exterior projector motion, and a
layered adjacent-pair gauge in either $SO(r)$ or $U(r)$. It supports any declared finite
$r\geq3$ and finite circuit depth, but every calculation remains bounded to a frozen
finite rank/depth ladder.

The global analytic frame makes the initial family topologically trivial. Closure,
Wilson-loop, and plaquette quantities are sewing and transport diagnostics, not
three-dimensional topological claims. A topologically obstructed model requires a
separate patchwise-frame protocol. Paper 5 owns later pristine/defect common-space
admission, and Paper 6 owns full obstruction studies; neither result is implied here.

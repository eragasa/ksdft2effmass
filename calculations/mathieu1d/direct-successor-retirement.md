# Mathieu1D calculation-code retirement

## Scientific question

For the finite Mathieu Hamiltonian

\[
H=-\frac{d^2}{dx^2}+0.5\cos(x),
\]

can the direct typed calculate → independently verify → authenticated render lifecycle
reproduce the finite numerical and plot-data channels needed from the calculation-local
predecessor without retaining its workflow-shaped producer, serializers, or live legacy
package?

The successor parity tests answer only that finite software question. They do not
establish continuum convergence, uncertainty quantification, material validity,
publication acceptance, evidence sealing, or a new retained execution.

## Disposition

The operator explicitly authorized retirement and modification of calculation-side
Periodic1D code, including the separate `calculations/mathieu1d/` root. The retirement
is limited to the ten code and test files listed below. It does not remove or rewrite
`runs/`, `schemas/`, `input.json`, evidence manifests, provenance, checksum catalogs,
reports, figures, protocol, or evidence policy.

The successor uses:

- typed Python-authored configuration and immutable typed result sections;
- canonical existing plane-wave, periodic finite-difference, Fourier-transform,
  truncation, fitting, interpolation, and diagnostic owners;
- a byte-owning independent direct-formula verifier;
- one authenticated typed visualization projection; and
- direct module-scope `run_01_calculation.py`, `run_02_verification.py`, and
  `run_03_render.py` adapters.

It does not import, wrap, subclass, or execute the retired calculation-local package.

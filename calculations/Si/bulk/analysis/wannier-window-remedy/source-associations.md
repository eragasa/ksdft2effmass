or a CSD0-passage record. Scope: the historical scalar-relativistic SSSP C6-B20-W21
source, unchanged AMN/U/U_dis, and the original blocked S110 gate. This is not admission

## Definition of done and present unresolved question

Establish the source, band/gauge, projection, point-order, geometry, unit/reference,
and uncertainty associations needed for a truthful verifier assertion. Persist that
assertion only if the review supports its complete meaning. Otherwise retain the
specific gap; neither equal hashes nor a declaration generated by the assertion
serializer establishes its truth.

The main outstanding question is **geometry identity**. The executed QE cell and the
nominal S30/Wannier90 cell are related by identifiable input serialization but are
not numerically identical. The current
[CSD0 protocol](../../research-protocol/controlled-reduction-protocol-hopping-first.tex)
requires an identity-level source/state/convention association and stops on a missing
or unequal member. This dossier does not choose a geometry identification, silently
waive that rule, or replace the source. The review must distinguish this question
from the supported common native-band lineage below.

## Projection semantics and the physical inner product

The maintained Simulations NNKP parser confirms this native order:

| Columns | Center (fractional) | Native labels |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | `(0,0,0)` | `l=-3`, `mr=1,2,3,4`, `r=1` |
| 5–8 | `(1/4,1/4,1/4)` | same four hybrids |
| 9 | `(0,0,0)` | `l=0`, `mr=1`, `r=2` |
| 10 | `(1/4,1/4,1/4)` | same additional radial s projection |

All ten use `z=(0,0,1)`, `x=(1,0,0)`, radial scale one; no bands are excluded.
The bound Wannier90 guide defines the four hybrids as half the combinations
`(s+px+py+pz)`, `(s+px-py-pz)`, `(s-px+py-pz)`, `(s-px-py+pz)`.
Thus the inverse half-Hadamard combinations recover the ordered `(s,px,py,pz)`
components at each center; native columns 9 and 10 supply their respective `s-star`
entries. The output order is the request's two center-major `(s,px,py,pz,s-star)`
blocks. `T` is an exact unitary trial-coordinate transformation and
`C = A T†`; this does **not** assert `C†C=I`.

`r=2` denotes the extra one-node radial trial. The package's zero-based radial-channel
label one is not a principal quantum number or evidence of a physical Si 4s orbital.
The center labels describe trial construction, not measured optimized Wannier centers
or orbital populations.

The Si UPF is **ultrasoft**, scalar-relativistic, not PAW:
`ae3aefd0811f9499dbc4a72f1f9ae02ef4fc7f3568bf6f559b68668719c69e2b`.
The QE 7.5 reference `compute_amn` branch explicitly computes `S|g>` with `s_psi`
for ultrasoft projections and then `evc† S g`. Its non-gamma-only scalar branch uses
that conjugate-transpose product; the retained XML has `gamma_only=false`.
Accordingly the interpretation is `A_mn=<psi_m|S|g_n>` in the QE generalized
pseudopotential metric, **not** a raw plane-wave L2 overlap or a claim about
all-electron orbital populations. In the modeled S-orthonormal parent-band frame,
coefficient-space inner products are Euclidean: no second S factor is inserted into
`W†C`. Actual solver/parent-state errors are not certified by these file checks.
The guiding-function source also explicitly says not to normalize the atomic Bloch
trials point by point; the raw-overlap scale must not be changed by whitening.

## Point and band ordering; disentanglement packing

The canonical WIN explicitly contains the 216-point order `(i/6,j/6,k/6)`, with
`i,j,k=0,...,5` and `k` varying fastest. Its full twelve-decimal point block matched
that declaration. NNKP is the eight-decimal presentation of this order. Parsed U and
U_dis point arrays match its ten-decimal presentation **exactly**. These comparisons
are to explicit declarations, not count-based inference, snapping, or reordered data.
AMN's integer point indices inherit this association through the converter lineage;
AMN itself has no coordinate records. Fractional coordinates and overlaps are
unitless. The physical Cartesian wavevectors also depend on the geometry below.

The native EIG parser confirms the complete ordered 216-by-20 table. NNKP excludes no
bands; thus its indices correspond to NSCF bands 1–20. The repeated QEXSD/EIG
association check used the QE constants source explicitly: the largest observed
energy difference was `5.009326287108706e-13 eV`; the retained converter's different
conversion route reports `5.364597654988756e-13 eV`. Neither observation is a new
rank gate or an independent numerical verification result.

Wannier90's `u_matrix_opt` uses **packed outer-window rows** and zeros unused rows.
Here EIG is ordered at every point, its global minimum is `-5.8341319732 eV`, and
`dis_win_min=-6 eV`. Hence the first included band is 1 everywhere: packed rows
coincide with the initial parent-band rows. Original window sizes are 12 at 48 points,
13 at 108, and 14 at 60; every stored U_dis entry after the corresponding window size
was checked to be zero. Therefore `W=U_dis U` in this source's parent-band coordinates
requires no row-offset expansion. This conclusion is **not transferable** to an
arbitrary lower window boundary or excluded-band configuration. Projection,
disentanglement, unitary basis change, and spatial truncation remain distinct.

## Geometry: nominal declaration versus executed rendering

With direct vectors as matrix **columns**, S30 records
`D=(a/2)*[[1,1,0],[0,1,1],[1,0,1]]`,
`a=5.469182093640207 angstrom`, with positive determinant. WIN lists `Dᵀ` as three
vectors, rounded to twelve decimals: every nonzero entry is `2.734591046820`.
Both atoms and trial centers use origin and the body-diagonal quarter site.

The [actual NSCF input](../../simulations/evidence/sssp-1.3.0-pbe-efficiency-qe-7.5-accepted-s1-wannier-nscf-6x6x6-b20-david-c6-b20/input/pw.in)
instead serializes nonzero cell entries to **eight decimals**, `2.73459105 angstrom`.
The authenticated NSCF XML
`2e1b5e157142d18210d18710fb9f5f3e813b17d8331acf96e11af9b9d4f7ef8c`
contains `5.167628147352816 bohr` for those entries. Using QE's explicit
`bohr=0.529177210903 angstrom` gives the executed rendering, with maximum difference
`3.1798967903284847e-9 angstrom` from the S30 nominal matrix. Using another constant
to force equality would be invalid. The S1 structure's nominal quarter-site floats
are also rendered to exact decimal quarters in QE/WIN.

QE's `read_nnkp` checks the supplied lattice with `eps6`, then transforms projection
centers with its own native `at`. That explains compatibility of this occurrence
without proving exact equality of the different lattice declarations. The actual
native reciprocal-order comparison, using the XML reciprocal coefficients and their
native `2pi/alat` convention, had residual `7.074341112911497e-13`; this too is an
association observation, not a declaration of exact physical wavevector equality
between the differently rendered cells.

The common native finite-band lineage can be documented without claiming that these
Cartesian geometry declarations are identical. Whether full CSD0 can use an explicit
nominal-to-executed serialization identification under the current protocol is the
open review question. No geometry identity is selected for a canonical assertion here;
no lattice, input, energy reference, or archived result has been modified. The small
magnitude alone is not authority to pass an identity-level gate.

## Spin, units, reference, and uncertainty assumptions

The retained XML declares `lsda=false`, `noncolin=false`, `spinorbit=false`; WIN has
`spinors=false`, and the converter selects `spin_component='none'`. “Spinless” here
means one scalar orbital block of the nonmagnetic spin-degenerate calculation, not a
doubled twenty-Wannier spinor basis or a claim that electrons have no spin.

EIG and HR use eV in the unchanged native reference. QE's `write_band` writes
`et*rytoev`, without a Fermi/valence shift. S30/S105/S110 must retain that reference;
this dossier performs no energy subtraction, scissor correction, or fitting.

The declared native component-error model is nearest absolute decimal rounding:
AMN `1/(2*10^12)`, U and U_dis `1/(2*10^10)`, plus correctly rounded IEEE binary64
parsing error as handled by the existing verifier. Source formats `2f18.12` and
`f15.10` support the stated decimal quanta. **Nearest rounding and source-to-build
correspondence remain declared assumptions**, not facts proved by decimal appearance
or these source hashes. The model bounds printing/parsing error relative to the
computed native matrices; it does not bound DFT, basis, solver, physical metric,
localization, geometry-rendering, or model-reduction errors. More arithmetic cannot
remove these distinctions. The frozen raw threshold is the exact rational encoding
of the original binary64 `5.890206739032098e-7`, not a normalized-overlap threshold.

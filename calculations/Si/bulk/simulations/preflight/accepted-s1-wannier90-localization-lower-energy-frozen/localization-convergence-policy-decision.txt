# Prospective Wannierisation convergence-policy decision

## Accepted recommendation

The scientific owner accepted the recommendation from
[`localization-convergence-policy-review.md`](localization-convergence-policy-review.md).
For a prospective successor using the exact two-atom primitive silicon cell, the active
Wannierisation optimizer-eligibility coordinates are:

```text
conv_tol = 2.0e-9 angstrom^2
conv_window = 6
```

The absolute tolerance is the AiiDA-precise-associated per-atom coordinate
`1.0e-9 angstrom^2/atom` multiplied by the explicitly bound two-atom cell. This scaling
convention must be recalculated and reviewed if the cell atom count changes.

## Scope

This decision:

- applies prospectively to a newly declared target-space or projection construction;
- requires the native Wannierisation convergence message;
- forbids accepting an occurrence that exhausts its declared iteration budget;
- treats the tolerance/window pair as an operational eligibility gate, not a physical
  constant or scientific validation;
- leaves the dimensionless disentanglement threshold `dis_conv_tol = 1.0e-10` unchanged;
- does not adopt the AiiDA precise 40000-iteration budget, k-point spacing, band factor,
  pseudopotential family, or other protocol settings; and
- does not authorize any calculator invocation.

## Historical disposition

All retained occurrences keep their exact historical inputs and the
`1.0e-10 angstrom^2`, window-3 policy under which they ran. None is retrospectively
reclassified. The conduction-frozen 4000-iteration occurrence also fails the newly
accepted precise operational gate and remains operator-ineligible.

The moderate comparison `2.0e-7 angstrom^2`, window 3 remains an exploratory workflow
reference only. It is not an accepted production gate for this route.

## Independent scientific acceptance

An eligible localized operator must still pass prospective construction- and
withheld-mesh tests for spectra, valley identity and location, edge energies,
derivatives, curvature/effective masses, and locality/tail sensitivity. Passing the
optimizer gate alone does not accept a Wannier operator.

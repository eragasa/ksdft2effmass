## First preprocessing realization

The recommended first preprocessing realization uses seed `silicon`, Wannier90 3.1.0,
and separate `Si:sp3` and `Si:l=0:r=2` source projection declarations without an
additional projection-selection map. This separation corrects the earlier compact
spelling: Wannier90 applies a trailing `:r=2` to every angular state on the same line,
which would incorrectly make the `sp3` hybrids nodal as well. No calculator consumed
the superseded compact spelling. The current ProjectKoios renderer requires an
explicit outer window when `num_wann < num_bands`; therefore the approved semantic
choice “all 12 retained bands” is represented as `[-5.85, 19.02] eV`. This interval
encloses the complete Davidson-QEXSD eigenvalue range
`[-5.834131973200469, 19.008744989627342] eV`. Its zero is the native QE Kohn–Sham
eigenvalue reference, not an aligned physical energy reference. No frozen window is
used.

Qualification controls are explicit rather than inferred from the tutorial:

- localization: 100 iterations, `1.0e-10 angstrom^2` tolerance, three-iteration
  convergence window;
- disentanglement: 200 iterations, `1.0e-10 angstrom^2` tolerance,
  three-iteration convergence window, and mixing ratio `0.5`; and
- `write_hr = .true.` for the later separately authorized localization occurrence.

These controls are first-candidate coordinates consistent with documented Wannier90
3.1.0 defaults except that localization convergence is explicitly enabled. They do
not establish scientific adequacy.

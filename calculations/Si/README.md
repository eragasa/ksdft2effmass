# Silicon calculations

This directory groups calculation owners whose physical subject is silicon. Material
identity does not transfer evidence between calculations: bulk, defect, spin-orbit, and
doped-supercell calculations require their own compatible state spaces, numerical
protocols, provenance, and verification.

- [`bulk/`](bulk/) owns planning, analysis, and technical reporting for qualification
  of the scalar-relativistic, non-spin-polarized, non-SOC bulk-silicon parent and its
  prospective spectral/operator reduction.

Historical tutorial and bootstrap records remain at `calculations/bulk-silicon/`
because their embedded paths and retained provenance are not rewritten by this
organization layer.

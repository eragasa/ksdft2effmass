\section{Scope and evidence boundary}

This report documents the finite mathematics, numerical conventions, and software-owner
boundary of the periodic one-dimensional higher-rank controlled calculation used by the
Paper C development program.  It is calculation-owned and publication-independent.  The
selected protocol remains the authority for coefficients, controls, tolerances, schemas,
and future retained evidence.  This report explains that contract; it does not replace
it.

The calculation-local \path{workflow/} package and its ignored summaries are replaceable
development material.  They are not a maintained runtime, retained run, convergence
result, or evidence package.  No result reported below was obtained from a new execution.
The labels D1, P0, and G0 are retained only where they identify the frozen protocol.  They
are not proposed public software class names.

\section{Finite model inventory}

The selected ladder uses retained ranks
\[
 r\in\{3,4,8,16\},
 \qquad L\in\{1,2,4,8\},
\]
ambient dimension $2r$, construction meshes $M\in\{513,1025,2049\}$, an
independent evaluation mesh of size $8191$, and hopping supports
$S\in\{0,1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128\}$.  All construction meshes are odd.  Their complete
centered translation inventories are therefore ordinary inversion-complete sets with no
self-inverse Nyquist representative.

Four alignment controls remain distinct: no alignment, one global unitary, pointwise
complete unitaries, and the known generating inverse.  The known inverse is an oracle
control and is not evidence of practical recoverability.  Transport of the analytic
reference frame precedes these alignment controls and is shared by them.

\section{State spaces and action conventions}

At each reciprocal coordinate, an ambient frame is a matrix
\[
 F(k)\in\mathbb C^{2r\times r},
 \qquad F(k)^\dagger F(k)=I_r.
\]
Its columns use one declared ambient basis and span a rank-$r$ retained space.  A retained
coordinate transformation acts on the right,
\[
 F(k)\longmapsto F(k)U(k),
 \qquad U(k)\in U(r),
\]
whereas the reciprocal-endpoint sewing map acts on the left in the ambient space,
\[
 F(-\pi)\longmapsto S_G F(-\pi),
 \qquad S_G\in\mathbb C^{2r\times2r}.
\]
For the selected P0 construction, $S_G=I_{2r}$, but the route still records and verifies
that declaration.  Identity sewing is not inferred from periodic eigenvalues or array
shape.

If $H_p(k)$ is the parent and $H_F(k)=F(k)^\dagger H_p(k)F(k)$ is its represented
Hamiltonian, then a right frame action gives the covariant matrix transformation
\[
 H_{FU}(k)=U(k)^\dagger H_F(k)U(k).
\]
Transport, alignment, and Fourier transformation therefore have ordered and generally
noncommuting roles.

\section{Sequential polar transport}

For one odd half-open mesh, let
\[
 k_n=-\pi+\frac{2\pi n}{M},
 \qquad n=0,\ldots,M-1,
\]
and append the direct endpoint $k_M=+\pi$.  Write the analytically sampled frame as
$Y_n=F(k_n)$, including $Y_M=F(+\pi)$.  Set $\widetilde F_0=Y_0$.  Given the transported
frame at index $n$, form
\begin{equation}
 O_n=\widetilde F_n^\dagger Y_{n+1}
    =L_n\Sigma_nR_n^\dagger,
 \qquad
 Q_n=R_nL_n^\dagger,
 \qquad
 \widetilde F_{n+1}=Y_{n+1}Q_n.
 \label{eq:technical-sequential-transport}
\end{equation}
This is the right-unitary Procrustes transformation that aligns the next raw frame toward
the already transported predecessor.  Indeed,
\[
 \widetilde F_n^\dagger\widetilde F_{n+1}
 =L_n\Sigma_nL_n^\dagger,
\]
which is Hermitian positive semidefinite.

The complete singular spectrum is retained for every attempted link.  With
\[
 \tau_{\mathrm{sv}}(O_n)
 =\max\!\left(10^{-12},10^{-10}\sigma_{\max}(O_n)\right),
\]
the link qualifies only when
$\sigma_{\min}(O_n)>\tau_{\mathrm{sv}}(O_n)$.  Equality is deficient.  If a neighbor
link is deficient, no null-space completion is applied: that link is recorded as
deficient and all links that depend on its transported output are recorded as
unattempted.  A backend decomposition failure is separate from mathematical rank
deficiency and carries a stable operation, reason, and link index.

\section{Endpoint mismatch, holonomy, and distributed closure}

After the endpoint step, define the protocol-oriented endpoint mismatch
\begin{equation}
 S=\widetilde F_0^\dagger\widetilde F_M.
 \label{eq:technical-endpoint-mismatch}
\end{equation}
Let its admitted unitary polar factor be
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal H
 =Z\operatorname{diag}(e^{i\gamma_1},\ldots,e^{i\gamma_r})Z^\dagger,
 \qquad \gamma_a\in(-\pi,\pi].
 \label{eq:technical-holonomy-spectrum}
\end{equation}
The branch is part of the method.  At eigenvalue $-1$, this convention chooses
$\gamma=+\pi$, not $-\pi$.  Both signs exponentiate to the same endpoint eigenvalue, but
they select different fractional matrix roots and therefore different intermediate
gauges.  The branch cannot be chosen after observing a locality outcome.

The periodically corrected frame is
\begin{equation}
 F_n^{\mathrm{pt}}
 =\widetilde F_n
 Z\operatorname{diag}\!\left(
 e^{-i(n/M)\gamma_1},\ldots,e^{-i(n/M)\gamma_r}
 \right)Z^\dagger,
 \qquad n=0,\ldots,M.
 \label{eq:technical-distributed-closure}
\end{equation}
The phases are stored deterministically for reproducibility, but their scientific meaning
is an unordered multiset.  Sorting is a storage convention, not a band labeling through
degeneracies.

\begin{proposition}[Finite endpoint closure]
If $\widetilde F_M=\widetilde F_0\mathcal H$ and
Equations~\eqref{eq:technical-holonomy-spectrum}--\eqref{eq:technical-distributed-closure}
hold, then $F_M^{\mathrm{pt}}=F_0^{\mathrm{pt}}$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
At $n=M$, the correction is
$Z\operatorname{diag}(e^{-i\gamma_a})Z^\dagger=\mathcal H^\dagger$.  Hence
\[
 F_M^{\mathrm{pt}}
 =\widetilde F_M\mathcal H^\dagger
 =\widetilde F_0\mathcal H\mathcal H^\dagger
 =\widetilde F_0
 =F_0^{\mathrm{pt}}.
\]
\end{proof}

For the selected P0 family, the analytic endpoint obeys
$Y_M=S_GY_0$ exactly.  In that setting the Optimization owner retains both orientations.
Its closure right action $\mathcal A$ aligns the last primitive frame toward the sewn
first frame,
\[
 \widetilde F_{M-1}\mathcal A\approx S_G\widetilde F_0,
\]
and its endpoint mismatch is $\mathcal E=\mathcal A^\dagger$, matching the protocol
orientation of $\mathcal H$.  Schur phases are phases of $\mathcal E$, not $\mathcal A$.
The owner correction step is
\[
 \mathcal W=Z\operatorname{diag}(e^{-i\gamma_a/M})Z^\dagger,
 \qquad \mathcal W^M=\mathcal E^\dagger=\mathcal A,
\]
and sample $j$ receives the right action $\mathcal W^j$.  The explicit owner branch
\texttt{negative\_pi\_open}, whose value is $(-\pi,\pi]$, therefore reproduces the
protocol directly, including $\gamma=+\pi$ and
$\mathcal W=e^{-i\pi/M}$ at eigenvalue $-1$.  An interface that stored phases of
$\mathcal A$ instead would require the inverse branch $[-\pi,\pi)$ to reproduce the same
path.

\begin{remark}
A Schur basis inside an exactly degenerate phase subspace need not be unique.  The matrix
function is nevertheless invariant under basis changes that remain within a subspace
assigned the same branch value.  Near degeneracy, backend-specific Schur vectors are
numerical evidence, not canonical physical band labels.
\end{remark}

\section{Metric-explicit diagnostics}

All scientific diagnostics name their norm and normalization.  For an $r$-column frame
or an $r\times r$ unitary, the protocol-normalized Frobenius defect is
\[
 d_{F,\mathrm{norm}}(A,B)
 =\frac{\lVert A-B\rVert_F}{\sqrt r},
 \qquad
 d_{U,\mathrm{norm}}(U)
 =\frac{\lVert U^\dagger U-I_r\rVert_F}{\sqrt r}.
\]
The reciprocal endpoint checks are
\[
 d_{\mathrm{frame,endpoint}}
 =\frac{\lVert F(+\pi)-F(-\pi)\rVert_F}{\sqrt r},
\]
\[
 d_{\mathrm{projector,endpoint}}
 =\frac{\lVert P(+\pi)-P(-\pi)\rVert_F}{\sqrt r},
 \qquad P=FF^\dagger,
\]
and the primitive closure check is
\[
 d_{\mathrm{closure}}
 =\frac{\lVert F_M^{\mathrm{pt}}-F_0^{\mathrm{pt}}\rVert_F}{\sqrt r}.
\]
The selected endpoint and primitive-closure tolerance is $10^{-11}$.  The P0 closure
phase gate is
\[
 d_{\mathrm{phase}}=\max_a|\gamma_a|\leq10^{-10}\ \mathrm{radian}.
\]
This is a protocol integrity check for the exactly trivial control, not a generic demand
that physical holonomy vanish.

A reusable numerical owner may additionally retain a maximum-absolute-entry defect for
self-validation.  Such a value is not interchangeable with a Frobenius defect and must
carry a metric-explicit name.  KSD computes and retains the protocol diagnostics through
the canonical norm owners rather than relabeling an owner-internal validation metric.

\section{Alignment and represented operators}

Transport chooses a coherent reference frame.  It does not by itself align an attacked
candidate.  The four protocol channels are subsequently applied to the candidate from
the right.  Pointwise and one-global Procrustes alignment solve different optimization
problems; the known inverse is an oracle; identity alignment performs no fit.  A
rank-deficient global aggregate makes only that restricted channel inapplicable.  A
rank-deficient pointwise overlap invalidates the selected P0 case because the analytic
construction predicts full rank.

The KSD disposition inventory is closed:
\begin{itemize}
 \item \path{qualified} means every required rank, unitary, stationarity, objective,
       positive-semidefinite, oracle, and independent-comparison gate passes;
 \item \path{aggregate-rank-deficient} is a bounded A1 result: no A1 transformation or
       downstream A1 locality value is emitted;
 \item \path{pointwise-rank-deficient} invalidates the complete P0 case;
 \item \path{alignment-numerically-unresolved} records nonfinite input or typed backend
       failure without emitting a transformation;
 \item \path{alignment-integrity-failed} records a nominally full-rank A1/A2 factor that
       fails a required integrity or independent-comparison gate; and
 \item \path{oracle-integrity-failed} records failure of A3 analytic unitarity, frame
       recovery, operator recovery, or independent reconstruction.
\end{itemize}
Stable backend codes distinguish nonfinite arithmetic, nonconvergence, and validation
failure; traceback text and host paths are not scientific fields.

Every admitted action $U_A(k)$ transforms the represented Hamiltonian before Fourier
conversion,
\[
 H_A(k)=U_A(k)^\dagger H_C(k)U_A(k).
\]
Aligning already truncated hopping blocks is a different operation and is not substituted
for reciprocal-space alignment.

\section{Odd-mesh Fourier representation and locality}

For odd $M$, the complete ordinary translation set is
\[
 \mathcal R_M
 =\left\{-\frac{M-1}{2},\ldots,\frac{M-1}{2}\right\}.
\]
For each applicable channel,
\[
 T_{A,M}(R)=\frac1M\sum_{n=0}^{M-1}e^{-ik_nR}H_A(k_n),
 \qquad
 H_{A,M}(k)=\sum_{R\in\mathcal R_M}e^{ikR}T_{A,M}(R).
\]
There is no Nyquist singleton and no cyclic-to-ordinary removal channel.  Support
selection is a separate KSD policy:
$\Omega_S=\{R:|R|\leq S\}$.  The complete transform, selected support, and deliberate
truncation remain separate records.

On the withheld mesh, the operator residuals are
\[
 e_{\mathrm{interp}}=H_{A,M}-H_A^{\mathrm{direct}},
 \qquad
 e_{\mathrm{trunc}}^{(S)}=H_{A,M}^{(S)}-H_{A,M},
\]
\[
 e_{\mathrm{total}}^{(S)}
 =H_{A,M}^{(S)}-H_A^{\mathrm{direct}}
 =e_{\mathrm{interp}}+e_{\mathrm{trunc}}^{(S)}.
\]
Only the pointwise residuals telescope.  Their scalar maxima are not added, and a
cancellation in the total channel is reported rather than called convergence.  For each
channel $x\in\{\mathrm{interp},\mathrm{trunc},\mathrm{total}\}$, cross-rank operator
summaries use, for example,
\[
 \epsilon_{F,\max}^{(x)}
 =\max_k\frac{\lVert e_x(k)\rVert_F}{\sqrt rE_{\mathrm{ref}}},
 \qquad
 \epsilon_{F,\mathrm{RMS}}^{(x)}
 =\left(\frac1{N_{\mathrm{eval}}}\sum_k
 \frac{\lVert e_x(k)\rVert_F^2}{rE_{\mathrm{ref}}^2}\right)^{1/2}.
\]
Ordered-spectrum errors are normalized by $E_{\mathrm{ref}}$.  These are separate from
spectral-norm perturbation evidence and from hopping-tail weights.

\section{Limitations and nonclaims}

The selected family is a topologically trivial synthetic control.  Closure phases are
transport and sewing diagnostics, not a Chern, polarization, or Wannier-center result.
The distributed closure is a smooth-gauge construction, not a localization optimum.  No
two- or three-dimensional higher-rank calculation exists.  Moving retained subspaces,
complex gauge phases, restricted-circuit fitting, patchwise frames, and material
specialization remain prospective.  Current work establishes neither continuum
convergence nor scientific validation.

# Legacy M1 source retirement

## Scope and authorization

After the direct rank-one Fourier-locality lifecycle was merged, the operator explicitly
authorized complete retirement of the installed M1 `periodic1d.isolated_band` surface.
The retirement removes eight tracked files: six installed source modules, the sole direct
external test consumer, and the obsolete Sphinx API page. It also removes the
`Periodic1DFourierHamiltonianToyModel` definition and all legacy M1 exports from the two
surviving source modules that contained them.

The replacement is not a compatibility facade. The direct family owns a different exact
controlled four-state parent, family-specific typed records, exact result bytes,
independent verification, authenticated visualization data, and three direct effect
adapters. Historical Architecture v2 pages remain as historical records.

## Scientific disposition

The predecessor's reciprocal transform, ordinary-support truncation, Hermiticity,
Parseval, direct-fit, and disjoint-evaluation behaviors were adopted or adapted through
canonical owners and the direct Fourier-locality lifecycle. Its cosine parent,
plane-wave and finite-difference parent-refinement channels, schema-v1 wire, Action
signature, typed-object verifier input, and historical calculation identity were
explicitly rejected in the reviewed donor crosswalk. Removing them does not relabel the
new parent or its finite results as historical M1 evidence.

The predecessor result and publication package remain Git-only provenance. This source
retirement creates no retained execution, evidence seal, scientific validation,
publication update, or release.

\section{Limitations and nonclaims}

The model is synthetic and uses an arbitrary positive energy scale.  It supplies no
material parametrization, uncertainty quantification, thermodynamic-limit result, or
continuum certificate.  Finite momentum and amplitude samples do not establish extrema
between samples.  A first-passing cutoff is conditional on the declared mesh, evaluation
set, norm, tolerance, and maximum tested range.

The restricted global $SO(2)$ alignment is not maximally localized Wannier gauge
optimization.  Pointwise unitary equivalence does not make two represented matrix fields
identical under the declared restricted comparison.  Conversely, representation-induced
hopping redistribution is not evidence of a changed physical band spectrum.

Lean formalization of related finite identities does not certify NumPy execution,
serialized JSON, plots, binary64 rounding, or the applicability of this model to a
material system.

\section{Simulation method}

The development calculation has three related layers.  A flat traceless rank-two limit
supports exact analytic correlation and residual identities.  A dispersive four-state
finite-range parent supplies a momentum-dependent retained subspace and transported
periodic frame.  A bounded parent homotopy separates representation-induced effects
from pre-existing parent dispersion and hopping locality.

Momentum calculations assemble the same Bloch parent on nested meshes; refinement does
not change the parent Hamiltonian.  Fourier blocks are evaluated with an explicit
shifted-mesh convention.  Even finite-cyclic data are converted to an ordinary-lattice
inventory by removing and separately reporting the centered Nyquist block.  Ordered
symmetric cutoffs are then evaluated on a disjoint momentum mesh.  Cyclic conversion,
finite interpolation, deliberate truncation, and total sorted-band effects remain
separate channels.

The versioned five-stage calculation entry points have not yet been created.  Existing
values remain development outputs of the maintained experiment rather than results
produced from this successor directory.

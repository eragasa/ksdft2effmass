\subsection{Array shapes and finite inventories}

At construction size $N$, the parent matrix field has shape $(N,4,4)$, ordered parent
eigenvalues have shape $(N,4)$, raw and transported frames have shape $(N,4,2)$, and
retained matrix fields have shape $(N,2,2)$.  These shapes are part of the operational
contract.  Arrays retained by domain objects are copied to canonical numerical dtypes and
made read-only.

For one qualified amplitude point, the scalar record contains the amplitude, maximum
band defect, splitting scale, harmonic maximum, normalized restricted correlation, two
thresholds, loss-reconstruction defect, reference and comparison non-onsite weights,
reference and comparison Parseval defects, and the paired reference/comparison hopping
powers over the complete cyclic displacement inventory.

For one completed truncation amplitude point, the record additionally contains reference
and comparison Nyquist powers, ordinary Hermiticity defects, direct spectral-equivalence
defect on the shifted mesh, four interpolation errors, and one range record for every
cutoff.  Each range record retains reference and comparison omitted powers and separate
reference truncation, comparison truncation, and total comparison matrix and spectral
errors.

\subsection{Closed scientific outcomes}

\paragraph{Parent qualification.}
Exactly one of external-gap failure, transport-backend failure, transport numerically
unresolved, transport-threshold failure, or qualified parent is retained.  The variants
carry different fields because the available scientific information differs.

\paragraph{Momentum refinement.}
Exactly one of qualification stopped, converged, or unresolved is retained.  Convergence
requires the declared final stable-refinement count; exhaustion is not encoded as a
successful result.

\paragraph{Adaptive amplitude refinement.}
Exactly one of qualification stopped, converged, levels exhausted, or evaluations
exhausted is retained.  Convergence requires both midpoint acceptance and disjoint
quarter-point validation.

\paragraph{Truncation.}
Exactly one of qualification stopped or completed is retained.  A stopped result cannot
carry fabricated cutoff consequences.

\paragraph{Aggregate verification.}
The component inventory is exactly parent family, momentum refinement, adaptive
amplitude, and truncation, plus the prescribed nested visualization-projection checks.
A missing, reordered, substituted, or additional component is rejected.

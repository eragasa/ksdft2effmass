\section{Development analysis and results}

The existing development analysis reports three bounded observations.  First, the smooth
momentum-dependent rotation preserves the complete retained spectrum pointwise while
redistributing Fourier hopping power.  Second, the exact flat-parent residual--tail
identity deforms systematically when non-onsite parent hopping is introduced.  Third,
on the declared sampled truncation inventory, reference and rotated representations can
reach the same sampled band-error target at different first-passing cutoffs.

These observations remain development calculations.  The momentum study is reported as
converged on its declared nested meshes, whereas the capped uniform amplitude
interpolation study remains unresolved.  The range calculations are finite sampled
decisions with a bounded cutoff inventory; they are not continuum extrema or universal
range laws.

Numerical tables and figures will be incorporated only after the simulation and analysis
wire contracts are migrated and independently verified from this calculation root.

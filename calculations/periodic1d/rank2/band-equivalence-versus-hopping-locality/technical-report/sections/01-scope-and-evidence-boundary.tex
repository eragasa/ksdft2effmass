\section{Scope and evidence boundary}

This report documents a controlled synthetic one-dimensional periodic calculation used
to study the distinction between pointwise band equivalence and hopping locality.  The
full parent has four states per cell; the isolated retained space has rank two.  The
calculation is not a Mathieu equation, a material Hamiltonian, a density-functional
calculation, or a scientific validation of a semiconductor model.

At this migration stage reusable numerical implementation remains with its Python owner,
while calculation-specific development producers, tables, and figures have been
extracted from the experiment into this calculation root.  This report does not promote
those development outputs into retained evidence.  Historical scalar and M1--M3
packages retain their original identities and evidence boundaries.

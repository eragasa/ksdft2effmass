\section{Model and conventions}

The parent is a one-dimensional finite-range Bloch Hamiltonian
\begin{equation}
 K(q)=\sum_{R=-1}^{1} e^{2\pi i qR}T_R,
 \qquad -\tfrac12\le q<\tfrac12,
\end{equation}
with four orbitals per cell and Hermitian reverse hopping
$T_{-1}=T_{+1}^{\dagger}$.  The two lowest eigenvectors are retained only when
the second-to-third-band isolation gate passes.  A discrete parallel-transport and
closure procedure supplies a periodic orthonormal frame $F(q)$, giving
\begin{equation}
 H(q)=F(q)^{\dagger}K(q)F(q).
\end{equation}

The band-equivalent comparison field is
\begin{align}
 \theta_a(q)&=a\sin(2\pi q),\\
 U_a(q)&=R(\theta_a(q)),\\
 A_a(q)&=U_a(q)^{\dagger}H(q)U_a(q),
 \qquad 0\le a\le\pi/4.
\end{align}
Thus $A_a(q)$ and $H(q)$ have identical ordered eigenvalues at every momentum, while
their Fourier hopping blocks in the declared retained coordinates generally differ.
The comparison admits one momentum-independent real rotation and deliberately excludes
reuse of $U_a(q)$.

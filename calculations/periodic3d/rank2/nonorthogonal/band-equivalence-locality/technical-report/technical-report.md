# Diamond-basis rank-two band-equivalence and locality: technical report

## Scope

This report covers the synthetic periodic three-dimensional nonorthogonal family with FCC Bravais geometry and a diamond basis. The retained operator has rank two. The calculation is silicon-inspired only at the controlled-geometry level and is not a bulk-silicon material calculation.

## Method

The bounded development and refinement entrypoints evaluate basis-aware support, band-equivalence, operator, and locality diagnostics and invoke the independent in-repository verifier. Plotting renders family, refinement, error-channel, and shell diagnostics.

## Evidence status

Generated JSON and figures are replaceable development output. No retained production run, checksum-bound package, first-principles input, or material validation is asserted.

## Interpretation boundary

The calculation supports a controlled nonorthogonal three-dimensional comparison. Chemical bonding, calibrated parameters, and experimental applicability remain outside scope.

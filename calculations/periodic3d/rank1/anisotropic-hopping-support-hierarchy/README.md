The calculation is deliberately anisotropic. It tests three-dimensional representation,
support nesting, witness, and certificate semantics without assuming simple-cubic axis
permutations, diamond-lattice shell equivalence, a silicon Wannier basis, or material
validity. Bulk silicon remains a separate material program.

## Ownership boundary

The eventual calculation owns its exact synthetic parent, lattice convention, support
classes, objective-construction and evaluation roles, thresholds, coefficient domain,
metric, certificate premises, strict schemas, independent verifier, retained-evidence
policy, and interpretation. Reusable finite-periodic transforms or unit-free numerical
kernels may be consumed directly from their reviewed owners; they do not own the
scientific composition.

No installed package may import this calculation tree. Nothing here authorizes Quantum
ESPRESSO, Wannier90, remote, cluster, production, retained, or confirmatory execution.

## Current gate

Implementation remains deferred until the earlier isotropic 3-D and anisotropic 2-D
stages have isolated their respective architecture stresses. Before this family can
receive an immutable version, review and freeze its protocol, implementation dependency
identities, strict input/result schemas, independent exact-rational verification route,
tests, and evidence-retention policy. Exploratory threshold calculations remain
identified as design work and separated from later sealed evaluation.

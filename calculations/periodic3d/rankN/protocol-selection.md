## Rank-two-derived realization

The recommended periodic-3D rank-N family mirrors the installed periodic-3D rank-two
scientific roles and lifecycle, not its fixed matrix sizes. It retains the orthogonal and
nonorthogonal lattice-family partition, exact `DirectLattice3D`/metric ownership,
family-owned bond and complete metric-shell inventories, separate ordinary-locality and
support-truncation channels, three-direction transport and all primitive plaquette
diagnostics, independent references, authenticated visualization data, and typed
lifecycle records.

Every rank-bound request and result must carry an explicit retained rank. Study-level
requests admit exactly `3`, `4`, and `8`; reusable pointwise Procrustes, transport,
locality, and support actions may admit another finite rank only through a separately
reviewed caller. Dimensional rank-N owners refine dimension-neutral periodic finite-rank
capabilities. Applicable `Periodic3DRank2...` operation owners then refine the
corresponding `Periodic3DRankN...` owner and fix the rank to two. The rank-N study family
does not inherit rank-two calculation policy, and its `3`, `4`, and `8` inventory remains
calculation-owned.

The recommended alignment inventory is identity, complete global-unitary Procrustes,
pointwise-unitary Procrustes, and a separately labelled known-inverse oracle when the
selected gauge construction supplies one. The rank-two global real-rotation restriction
is not generalized by renaming it. Restricted-circuit optimization remains out of scope.
Global and pointwise fitted alignments use the Project Koios `MAXIMUM` rank-cutoff rule,
rank components `1e-12` absolute and `1e-10` relative, and backend-validation components
`1e-12` absolute and `1e-10` relative. These are software-qualification controls, not
scientific overlap thresholds or convergence evidence.

The selected parent has ambient dimension `2 * retained_rank`, a rank-dimensional
retained seed, and separated retained/exterior sectors. The exact rank-dependent energy,
gap, generator, harmonic, and attack formulas are frozen in `recommended-baseline.md`
and implemented by the dimension-owned analytic model.

## Recommended review sequence

1. Select and certify the parent family and retained state space before choosing a gauge
   attack.
2. Select the lattice and physical metric before defining locality or support.
3. Select the gauge group, generators, depth semantics, and oracle boundary before
   specifying alignment actions.
4. Specify three-direction neighbor links, primitive closures, the three plaquette
   orientations, and any cell-level diagnostic before mesh and transport tolerances.
5. Select reciprocal meshes and the complete metric-shell support inventory together so
   that memory and transform costs remain explicit.
6. Freeze independent references, numerical gates, resource ceilings, and failure
   outcomes before observing protocol outcomes.
7. Review the rank-first public owner map before adding any Python class.
8. Implement and software-test the protocol without creating retained evidence.
9. Require a clean exact revision and separate authorization before any direct execution.

this fixed-geometry record transfers no outcomes or evidence.

\section{Direct Bain path and units}

The direct-length unit identity is
\path{synthetic-bain-direct-length-v1}, denoted $\ell_0$.  Direct vectors carry units
$\ell_0$, the direct metric carries $\ell_0^2$, primitive volume carries $\ell_0^3$,
and reciprocal vectors carry $\ell_0^{-1}$.  Unit primitive volume means
$V/\ell_0^3=1$; it is not one cubic metre, one cubic \AA, or a material lattice
constant.  A future typed geometry record must bind the unit identity to the numerical
components supplied to \texttt{DirectLattice3D}.

Let
\begin{equation}
 s\in[0,1],\qquad
 \gamma(s)=\frac ca=\sqrt2+s(1-\sqrt2),
\end{equation}
with both endpoints included.  Define the positive scales
\begin{equation}
 t(s)=\left(\frac{2}{\gamma(s)}\right)^{1/3},
 \qquad
 l(s)=\gamma(s)t(s)=\frac{2}{t(s)^2}.
\end{equation}
The required floating-point evaluation form is
\texttt{l = 2 / t**2}.  In fixed orthogonal Cartesian axes, the direct primitive
vectors are the columns
\begin{align}
 \mathbf a_1(s)&=\frac{\ell_0}{2}(t,t,l)^{\mathsf T},\\
 \mathbf a_2(s)&=\frac{\ell_0}{2}(-t,t,l)^{\mathsf T},\\
 \mathbf a_3(s)&=\frac{\ell_0}{2}(t,-t,l)^{\mathsf T},
\end{align}
and
\begin{equation}
 A_B(s)=[\mathbf a_1(s)\ \mathbf a_2(s)\ \mathbf a_3(s)].
\end{equation}
The primitive lattice basis is nonorthogonal.  Numerical Cartesian components are not
physical distances without the declared unit and metric.

The direct metric and reciprocal primitive matrix are
\begin{equation}
 G_B(s)=A_B(s)^{\mathsf T}A_B(s),
 \qquad
 K_B(s)=2\pi A_B(s)^{-\mathsf T},
 \qquad
 A_B(s)^{\mathsf T}K_B(s)=2\pi I_3.
\end{equation}
Reduced coordinates are dimensionless columns:
\begin{equation}
 \mathbf x=A_B(s)\mathbf n_B,
 \quad \mathbf n_B\in\ZZ^3,
 \qquad
 \mathbf k=K_B(s)\mathbf q_B,
 \quad \mathbf q_B\in[0,1)^3.
\end{equation}
A fixed $\mathbf q_B$ denotes one fixed point of the abstract reduced reciprocal torus,
not one fixed Cartesian wave vector as $s$ changes.

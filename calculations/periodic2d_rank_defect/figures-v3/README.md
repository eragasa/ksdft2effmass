# Protocol figure atlas, version 3

These 15 deterministic PDF/PNG pairs are produced by
`../render_research_protocol_figures.py` with no-overwrite behavior. The renderer
authenticates the retained folding/null result, the unexecuted version-2 finite-domain
inventory, and the calculated parent-qualification result, execution record, and
independent verification before rendering.

Version 3 contains all figures currently supportable from authenticated retained
numerical evidence or frozen execution-free planning geometry:

- the ten version-2 folding/null and planning figures;
- a complete normalized parent/perturbation qualification metric atlas;
- inherited radius-18 parent truncation losses;
- uniform-link to quotient-seam gauge-bridge residuals;
- the aggregate production-versus-independent-reconstruction comparison; and
- measured calculation and verification resources, explicitly without a scaling fit.

The parent qualification figures describe only the three exact accepted historical
**dimensionless synthetic** parents and two frozen perturbation inventories. They do not
establish material validation, finite-domain convergence, isolated-defect behavior,
uncertainty quantification, or authorization for the 3,240 proposed defect evaluations.

The remaining blocked protocol atlas items require defect operators, spectra,
eigenstates, model-discrimination results, adjacent-size comparisons, or accepted
absolute isolation thresholds. They are intentionally not fabricated here. Versions 1
and 2 under `../figures/` and `../figures-v2/` remain immutable.

## Case inventory retained from rank two

The periodic-2D rank-N family should retain the rank-two geometry partition:

1. square orthogonal control with bonds `((1, 0), (0, 1))`, axis-maximum gauge
   normalization, construction size `16`, evaluation size `33`, shift `1/3`, and shell
   cap `25`;
2. matched square Bravais control with the same square metric and bonds, bond-RMS gauge
   normalization, and shell cap `21`;
3. triangular Bravais control with primitive vectors
   \((1,0)\) and \((1/2,\sqrt{3}/2)\), bonds
   `((1, 0), (0, 1), (1, -1))`, bond-RMS normalization, and shell cap `21`; and
4. triangular-lattice two-basis metric control with offsets
   `((0, 0), (1/3, 1/3))`, the same triangular bonds, and shell cap `21`.

The fourth case uses basis offsets in physical-metric shell classification. The proposed
rank-N parent below is not a honeycomb tight-binding or material model, so the case should
be named as a basis-metric control rather than as graphene.

## Analytic parent

Let \(q\in[0,1)^2\) be reduced reciprocal coordinates and let \(B\) be the case's
positive-bond inventory. Define

\[
c_B(q)=\frac1{|B|}\sum_{b\in B}
\left[\frac34\cos(2\pi q\cdot b)+\frac14\cos(4\pi q\cdot b)\right].
\]

For orbital index \(j=0,\ldots,r-1\), define

\[
\xi_j=\frac{2j-r+1}{r-1},
\]

\[
e^-_j(q;\lambda)=0.4\xi_j+
0.25\lambda(1+0.2\xi_j)c_B(q),
\]

\[
e^+_j(q;\lambda)=3.0+0.3\xi_j+0.1\lambda c_B(q).
\]

The ambient basis is the ordered direct sum of `r` retained orbitals and `r` exterior
orbitals. The parent is

\[
H_{r,B}(q;\lambda)=
\operatorname{diag}(e^-_0,\ldots,e^-_{r-1},e^+_0,\ldots,e^+_{r-1}),
\]

with reference frame \(F_0=[I_r\;0]^\mathsf T\), dimensionless energy unit, and a fixed
zero. The proposed `norm_energy_scale` is `1.0`.

Because \(|c_B(q)|\leq1\) and \(0\leq\lambda\leq1\), the retained energies are at most
`0.7` and the exterior energies are at least `2.6`; the family therefore has the analytic
external-gap lower bound `1.9`. Adjacent retained energies remain ordered because their
coefficient derivative is at least `0.35`. These are properties of the proposed formulas,
not calculated outcomes.

The un-attacked retained operator has exact finite Fourier support on the onsite block and
translations generated by \(B\) and \(2B\). No finite support is assigned to the attacked
operator.

## Rank-N gauge circuit

For layer \(\ell=1,\ldots,L\), define

\[
s_{B,\ell}(q)=N_B\sum_{b\in B}
\left[\frac34\sin(2\pi q\cdot b)+
\frac14(-1)^\ell\sin(4\pi q\cdot b)\right],
\]

where \(N_B=1/2\) for the axis-maximum square control and
\(N_B=\sqrt{2/|B|}\) for bond-RMS cases. For adjacent-pair index
\(p=1,\ldots,r-1\), let

\[
w_{r,p}=\frac{1+0.15(2p-r)/(r-1)}{1.15},
\qquad
\theta_{\ell,p}(q)=A(-1)^{\ell+p}w_{r,p}s_{B,\ell}(q).
\]

Odd layers act on disjoint pairs `(0,1), (2,3), ...`; even layers act on
`(1,2), (3,4), ...`. Each pair action is the real Givens rotation

\[
R(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta&-\sin\theta\\
\sin\theta&\cos\theta\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The ordered product \(G_{r,L,B}(q)\in SO(r)\) acts on frames from the right. The attacked
frame and represented operator are

\[
F'(q)=F_0G_{r,L,B}(q),\qquad
H'(q)=G_{r,L,B}(q)^\dagger D_r(q)G_{r,L,B}(q),
\]

where \(D_r=\operatorname{diag}(e^-_0,\ldots,e^-_{r-1})\). The construction is smooth,
periodic, exactly invertible, and topologically trivial. The known-inverse oracle is
\(G^\dagger\). Although the attack lies in `SO(r)`, global and pointwise alignment should
use complete right `U(r)` Procrustes so their rank and residual evidence remain explicit.

Each case should evaluate exactly:

- `identity`;
- `global-unitary` selected once from the final construction mesh;
- `pointwise-unitary` selected independently at every mesh point; and
- `known-inverse-oracle`, labelled as a construction oracle rather than fitted recovery.

Transport should retain both primitive neighbor directions, both primitive closures, and
the primitive-axis-pair plaquette diagnostic. Rank-deficient links make the affected

\(rE_{\mathrm{norm}}^2\). Ordered-spectrum maximum errors are normalized by

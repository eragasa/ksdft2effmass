# QHO1D evidence policy

The direct QHO1D calculation is an illustrative finite-dimensional numerical study.
Its authenticated subjects are the typed calculation result, independent verification
record, and derived rendering record for one explicit execution.

Passing verification establishes only the declared software and binary64 numerical
comparisons. It does not establish:

- continuum convergence of the real-line oscillator approximation;
- separation of finite-box, boundary, and grid-discretization error;
- physical uncertainty quantification;
- semiconductor-model adequacy;
- scientific validation, publication, release, or human acceptance.

Figures and compiled reports are derived presentation artifacts. An execution is not a
sealed evidence package unless a separately authorized seal binds every admitted byte,
source identity, dependency identity, and claim boundary. No seal stage is implemented
by the current direct lifecycle.

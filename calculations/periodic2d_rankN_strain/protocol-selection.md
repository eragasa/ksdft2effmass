# Periodic-2D rank-N synthetic-strain protocol-selection record

**Status: SCIENTIFIC PROTOCOL UNSELECTED; EXECUTION AND RETAINED OUTPUT PROHIBITED**

## Determination

The repository does not contain one closed periodic-2D rank-N synthetic-strain
protocol. Two existing calculation owners cover noncomposable scopes:

1. [`calculations/periodic2d/rankN/`](../periodic2d/rankN/README.md) selects a
   fixed-geometry analytic software baseline and bounded independent verification, but
   no continuous deformation coordinate or strain-response law.
2. [`calculations/periodic2d_rank2_rhombic_path/`](../periodic2d_rank2_rhombic_path/README.md)
   owns bounded rank-two rhombic M0/M1/M2 and sampled-scan development controls, but its
   four-state parent, response formulas, meshes, tolerances, and outputs are rank-two
   contracts.

Neither owner authorizes a rank-N strain calculation. This package therefore records the
separate scientific boundary without manufacturing a protocol from those donors.

## Required selections

A reviewed protocol must explicitly select, before implementation or execution:

- the exact unit-bearing direct-lattice path, orientation, reciprocal convention,
  endpoint identities, basis offsets, and common-cell map;
- the ambient parent state space and its exact relation to represented rank;
- separate geometry-only, frozen-parent, and prescribed-response channels, including
  which channels are scientifically applicable;
- calculation-owned rank, circuit-depth, parent, gauge-transformation, and path-sample
  inventories;
- same-strain and cross-strain alignment and frame-transport operands;
- Fourier, descriptive locality, complete-shell support, and irreversible truncation
  conventions under the varying metric;
- separate parent-model, finite-sampling, interpolation, truncation, and total error
  operands;
- independent references and typed unresolved or inapplicable outcomes;
- numerical gates, resource bounds, authenticated visualization, lifecycle,
  no-overwrite identity, provenance, and retained-evidence policy.

Rank two is included in the reusable finite-rank capability boundary, but the fixed
study inventory of another calculation does not automatically admit it or exclude it
here. No structured basis identity may be inferred from rank, geometry, support, or a
string label.

## Admission gate

This context remains documentation-only until one reviewed protocol closes every item
above and binds each compared operator to an explicit state space, ordered basis, gauge,
energy unit and zero, geometry, and reciprocal-sample identity. Software tests may then
establish implementation behavior only; they will not establish scientific validation,
convergence, or material-strain relevance.

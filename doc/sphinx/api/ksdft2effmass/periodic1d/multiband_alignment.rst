M2 multiband alignment and locality
===================================

M2 transports a rank-two frame, applies a controlled momentum-dependent gauge attack,
and keeps invariant projector/spectral diagnostics distinct from frame-dependent
represented-matrix and finite-range locality diagnostics.

Pointwise Procrustes recovery chooses a unitary at every momentum. The constrained
channel chooses one global unitary. These are different feasible families and their
results must not be conflated.

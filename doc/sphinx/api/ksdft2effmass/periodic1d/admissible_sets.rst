M3 constrained admissible sets
==============================

M3 composes the exact rank-two M2 baseline and derives angle-resolved quadratics over
a continuous normalized rectangle using a frozen family of nine global rotations. A
compatible disposition requires the prospective common witness. A separated disposition
requires a splitting-axis lower bound above the declared resolution; failure of the
prospective witness alone cannot prove separation.

Thresholds are prospective pedagogical controls for the synthetic benchmark. They are
not physical or uncertainty-calibrated tolerances. The separate threshold-sensitivity
chart is post hoc and cannot alter the sealed confirmatory result.

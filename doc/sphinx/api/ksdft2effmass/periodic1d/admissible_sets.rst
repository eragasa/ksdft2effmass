M3 constrained admissible sets
==============================

M3 consumes exact canonical
:class:`~ksdft2effmass.periodic1d.composite_band_alignment.Periodic1DCompositeBandAlignmentCalculationResult`
bytes and authenticates them through the explicit upstream verifier. It derives
angle-resolved quadratics over a continuous normalized rectangle using a frozen family
of nine global rotations. A compatible disposition requires the prospective common
witness. A separated disposition requires a splitting-axis lower bound above the
declared resolution; failure of the prospective witness alone cannot prove separation.

Thresholds are prospective pedagogical controls for the synthetic benchmark. They are
not physical or uncertainty-calibrated tolerances. Historical M2 and M3 artifacts remain
provenance references and are not decoded by this runtime. The separate historical
threshold-sensitivity chart is post hoc and cannot alter a confirmatory result.

v0.1.0 numerical-tolerance register
===================================

Status and decision boundary
----------------------------

This is the proposed human-review register for the release-facing numerical
acceptance criteria in the software-only ``v0.1.0`` claim set.  It reviews the
implementation and retained evidence inherited from
``606e7813d6de046faf57cfa43c82c9be47d7acd4`` together with the narrowly scoped
relative-tolerance correction described under ``TOL-13``.  The final immutable
review commit remains unresolved until that correction and this register complete
review.  This document does not approve the release, validate a physical model,
quantify scientific uncertainty, or authorize merge, tagging, or publication.

Human review accepted ``TOL-01`` through ``TOL-16`` for the bounded finite
software and numerical claims stated here, including the ``TOL-13`` correction.
This acceptance is not release approval or scientific acceptance.  A passing test
is evidence that a value is inside a declared envelope; it is not evidence that the
envelope is scientifically adequate.  Structural properties—including identities,
hashes, shapes, ordering, units, finiteness, Hermiticity channels,
unavailable-channel inventories, and exact-zero cases—remain exact and cannot be
repaired or hidden by increasing a numerical tolerance.

Scope and comparison convention
-------------------------------

The register includes each nonzero acceptance envelope or conditioning threshold
that directly supports the bounded numerical-verification claim, plus the retained
particle-in-a-box verifiers and synthetic native-Wannier90 verifier exercised by the
release suite.  These are the release-facing comparisons behind INC-03 through
INC-05 in :doc:`v0.1.0-claim-inventory`.

Unless a row states otherwise, NumPy closeness means

.. math::

   |x-y| \leq a_{\mathrm{tol}} + r_{\mathrm{tol}} |y|.

The binary64 machine epsilon is
``eps = 2.220446049250313e-16``.  Values described as normalized ``E_G`` are
represented with ``E_G = 1`` and therefore use the repository's ``Unitless``
quantity type.  Synthetic policy boundaries that are exactly representable binary
fractions are catalogued because they are named tolerances, but they are not
floating-point error envelopes.

Core and synthetic numerical evidence
-------------------------------------

``TOL-01`` — Geometry linear-independence threshold
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** ``sigma_min / sigma_max > 1e-12``; dimensionless and strict.
* **Scale and conditioning:** this admits represented cells with a binary64
  singular-value condition number below ``1e12`` and rejects equality.  Tests cover
  analytical diagonal, triangular, dependent, uniformly scaled, and row-permuted
  ``3 x 3`` cases over normal scales ``1e-200`` through ``1e200``.
* **Oracle:** exact singular values for diagonal cases and analytical linear
  dependence or triangular rank arguments; the test oracle does not call SVD.
* **Rationale:** the threshold is an explicit software precondition that keeps
  inversion-like downstream operations away from numerical rank loss.  It is not a
  forward-error theorem or a crystallographic cell-quality criterion.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept for the public
  represented-geometry invariant only.  Do not interpret admission as physical cell
  validity or adequacy for a calculation.

``TOL-02`` — Harmonic-oscillator analytical pullback
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** ``rtol = 8e-15`` and ``atol = 2e-15``; dimensionless normalized
  coordinates and Hamiltonian values.
* **Scale and conditioning:** one normalized three-vector and one ``3 x 3``
  hand-assembled matrix with entries of order one to four.  The bounds are about
  ``36 eps`` relative and ``9 eps`` absolute.
* **Oracle:** literal coordinates, an analytically normalized sampled Gaussian, the
  explicit tridiagonal matrix, direct scalar contraction, and exact reference energy
  ``0.5``.
* **Rationale:** the envelope covers the short sequence of exponential,
  normalization, matrix-vector, and scalar-reduction roundoff without admitting a
  percent-scale modeling discrepancy.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept for this single finite
  representation only.  Continuum convergence, larger retained spaces, and harmonic
  model adequacy remain unclaimed.

``TOL-03`` — Hermiticity residual analytical cases
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** nonzero expected residuals use
  ``64 eps * abs(expected)`` (relative factor
  ``1.4210854715202004e-14``) in ``eV``; analytical zero remains exact.
* **Scale and conditioning:** five explicitly prepared small ``complex128`` matrices;
  nonzero references include ``sqrt(26)``, ``1``, and ``1/sqrt(3)``.
* **Oracle:** entrywise hand derivations of
  ``max(abs(H - H.conjugate().T))``; the production analyzer is not reused.
* **Rationale:** ``64 eps`` is a conservative small-matrix regression allowance for
  subtraction, complex absolute value, and maximum reduction.  It is not a bound for
  arbitrary dimensions.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept for the stated
  synthetic cases.  It does not select a production Hermiticity tolerance, establish
  basis adequacy, or validate a Hamiltonian.

``TOL-04`` — Operator-residual analytical norms
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** nonzero references use ``64 eps * abs(expected)`` in ``eV``;
  analytical zero remains exact.
* **Scale and conditioning:** six matrices no larger than ``2 x 2``.  References are
  ``4`` or ``5`` and rank-one values ``1e-200`` and ``1e200``; subnormal acceptance is
  excluded.
* **Oracle:** hand-derived maximum-entry, Frobenius, and spectral norms for zero,
  diagonal ``3-4``, scalar ``3+4i``, and rank-one matrices.
* **Rationale:** the relative envelope covers scaled norm accumulation and the small
  SVD route while remaining strictly less than each nonzero expected magnitude, so
  an erroneous zero cannot pass.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept as a small-matrix
  binary64 regression criterion only, not as a universal norm-error theorem or an
  acceptable physical residual.

``TOL-05`` — Aligned-band synthetic boundary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** inclusive absolute policy ``0.25 hartree``; lattice and cutoff
  metadata tolerances are exactly zero.
* **Scale and conditioning:** two ``2 x 2`` spectra with values from ``0`` to ``3``
  hartree.  Every difference is a binary-exact multiple of ``0.125``.
* **Oracle:** the four literal absolute differences are ``0.125``, ``0.125``,
  ``0.25``, and ``0.25`` hartree.
* **Rationale:** this deliberately large value tests maximum traversal and equality at
  an inclusive caller-owned boundary.  It is not a roundoff allowance.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept solely as synthetic
  branch evidence.  It is not a recommended cross-code or materials-energy tolerance.

``TOL-06`` — Finite-sequence stopping boundary
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** inclusive absolute policy ``0.25`` in
  ``synthetic_energy_unit``.
* **Scale and conditioning:** exact binary-fraction changes ``0.5`` followed by
  ``0.25`` between three scalar values near ``-10``.
* **Oracle:** direct subtraction of the literal represented values.
* **Rationale:** the value tests the exact inclusive stopping rule and candidate
  selection; no approximate comparison is involved.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept as algorithmic boundary
  evidence only.  It is not a production convergence criterion.

Retained periodic-one-dimensional evidence
------------------------------------------

``TOL-07`` — Composite retained reconstruction
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** inclusive absolute ``1e-11`` in normalized ``E_G`` units.
* **Scale and conditioning:** rank-two matrices on a 128-point reciprocal mesh with
  finite Fourier/inverse sums, Hermitian eigenspectra, and direct complex least
  squares.  The largest bounded defect in the local review recomputation was
  ``3.21054467947917e-14``; large rough-versus-smooth physical differences are
  diagnostics and are not tolerance-masked.
* **Oracle:** exact retained hashes and controls, independently coded direct finite
  sums, dense Hermitian eigenspectra, and ``numpy.linalg.lstsq``.  The verifier does
  not import the production transform, fit, or frame algorithms.
* **Rationale:** the envelope is conservative relative to binary64 operation counts
  across 128 samples and the independent eigensolver/least-squares routes while
  remaining small relative to the normalized order-one energy scale.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept for the explicitly
  reconstructable channels.  Eight channels lacking sufficient retained source data
  remain unavailable rather than passing by tolerance.  No topology, polarization,
  or material claim follows.

``TOL-08`` — Isolated retained ordinary channels
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** inclusive absolute ``1e-10`` in normalized ``E_G`` units.
* **Scale and conditioning:** plane-wave cutoff 11 (``23 x 23`` matrices), 64-point
  transform mesh, 257-point withheld mesh, dense Hermitian eigensolves, finite sums,
  and least squares.  The largest ordinary defect in the local review recomputation
  was ``2.9753977059954195e-13``.
* **Oracle:** independently assembled cosine-potential matrices, SciPy Mathieu
  characteristic values, direct Fourier sums, and direct least squares.
* **Rationale:** the envelope covers accumulated cross-backend binary64 eigensolver,
  transform, and reduction differences across the retained finite campaign and is
  small relative to the order-one normalized energy scale.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept for the listed retained
  channels only.  Gauge-transport/overlap and localization-profile channels lack
  retained source values and remain unavailable.

``TOL-09`` — Isolated zone-center curvature
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** inclusive absolute ``1e-7`` in normalized ``E_G`` per squared
  reduced-momentum unit, represented as ``Unitless``.
* **Scale and conditioning:** the second difference uses ``h = 1e-3`` and divides
  ``E(-h) - 2 E(0) + E(h)`` by ``h**2 = 1e-6``.  The cancellation therefore
  amplifies low-eigenvalue backend differences by roughly ``1e6``.  The local review
  recomputation defect was ``6.94999613415348e-8``.
* **Oracle:** three independently assembled ``23 x 23`` plane-wave eigenspectra and
  the explicit centered second-difference formula.
* **Rationale:** this channel is separated from the ``1e-10`` ordinary envelope
  because its fixed-step subtraction is inherently cancellation-sensitive.  A change
  in step, cutoff, eigensolver, or curvature formula requires renewed review rather
  than automatic reuse of ``1e-7``.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept narrowly as a portable
  regression envelope for this represented diagnostic.  It is not a truncation-error
  estimate, derivative convergence study, uncertainty bound, or effective-mass
  validation.

``TOL-10`` — Stress-campaign stable scalar channels
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** inclusive absolute ``1e-10`` in normalized ``E_G`` units.
* **Scale and conditioning:** potential strengths ``0`` through ``4``, plane-wave
  cutoffs 5 through 15, meshes 8 through 128, a 257-point withheld mesh, and direct
  eigensolver, Fourier, gauge-covariance, and least-squares reconstructions.  The
  local review recomputation reported zero for the five aggregated stable-channel
  maxima.
* **Oracle:** independent dense plane-wave and finite-difference assembly, direct
  finite sums, projector identities, and complex least squares from exact correlated
  controls.
* **Rationale:** the order ``1e-10`` envelope is a conservative binary64 portability
  bound across many finite reductions and decompositions on normalized order-one
  data.  It does not relax exact controls or discrete dispositions.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept for stable represented
  stress channels only.  The campaign is illustrative and supports no transferability
  or material-adequacy claim.

``TOL-11`` — Stress-campaign isolated sewn overlaps
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:**
  ``max(1e-10, 2 * sqrt(eps)) = 2.9802322387695312e-8``; dimensionless overlap
  magnitude.
* **Scale and conditioning:** normalized single-band eigenvectors after explicit
  sign/phase reconciliation.  Eigenvector directions become conditioning-sensitive
  as spectral isolation narrows, unlike eigenvalues and stable scalar reductions.
* **Oracle:** independent dense eigensolves, reciprocal sewing, and scalar parallel
  transport.  The local isolated-channel defect was zero.
* **Rationale:** ``sqrt(eps)`` is a deliberately separate eigenvector-portability
  scale, not an energy tolerance.  It remains far below order-one overlap magnitude.
  The verifier applies it only where the exact retained isolation disposition is
  true.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept for isolated synthetic
  overlaps only.  All 65 nonisolated single-band overlap channels remain exactly
  unavailable; the bound cannot make them pass and is not a general eigenvector
  perturbation theorem.

``TOL-12`` — Synthetic native-Wannier90 Wilson loop
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** phase absolute tolerance ``1e-12 radian``; loop-unitarity
  absolute tolerance ``1e-12`` dimensionless; minimum-overlap singular-value
  threshold ``0.5`` dimensionless.
* **Scale and conditioning:** one rank-two, one-point loop with exact eigenvalues
  ``-i`` and ``+i``, identity native gauge, and singular value ``1``.
* **Oracle:** analytical phases ``-pi/2`` and ``+pi/2``, exact identity-gauge
  equivalence, and direct loop multiplication.
* **Rationale:** ``1e-12`` allows eigendecomposition and phase-extraction roundoff;
  ``0.5`` is a deliberately loose synthetic conditioning guard whose represented
  overlap is exactly twice the threshold.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept as synthetic parser and
  loop-assembly evidence only.  It does not verify an external Wannier90 execution,
  a retained production run, topology, or material behavior.

Retained particle-in-a-box verifier evidence
--------------------------------------------

``TOL-13`` — Core retained particle-in-a-box result
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** spectrum-ratio ``rtol = 16 eps``; computed-spectrum
  ``rtol = 64 eps`` and ``atol = 64 eps * max(expected spectrum)``; matrix identities
  use explicit ``rtol = 0`` and ``atol = 256 eps * ||H||_F``.  The retained
  eight-point dimensionless case has
  ``||H||_F = 274.68436431657335``, giving matrix ``atol``
  ``1.5613998375361766e-11``.
* **Scale and conditioning:** an ``8 x 8`` symmetric tridiagonal Hamiltonian and a
  three-dimensional retained eigenspace; the maximum exact discrete energy is
  ``157.11510228365856``.
* **Oracle:** closed-form Dirichlet eigenvalues, explicit sine eigenvectors,
  orthogonality, projector identities, compressed coordinates, and exact boundary
  residual structure.
* **Rationale:** epsilon-scaled bounds track the represented matrix and spectrum
  magnitude and cover eigensolver, matrix-product, and reduction operation counts.
  Review found that the matrix calls previously inherited NumPy's undeclared default
  ``rtol = 1e-7``.  The candidate now sets ``rtol = 0`` explicitly and includes a
  regression test proving that a ``1e-8`` order-one projector perturbation is rejected.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept for the retained finite
  illustrative result.  The finite matrix is not the continuum operator and does not
  establish semiconductor evidence.

``TOL-14`` — Particle-in-a-box convergence and eigenpair sweeps
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** reconstructed relative-error allowance combines the retained
  closed-form error with ``32 eps`` or a ``128 eps`` spacing/energy scale; stored
  convergence orders use ``rtol = atol = 2e-10``; nodal overlap, scaled residual,
  and relative discarded-sector checks require values below ``1e-13``.  Reported
  final-order windows are ``(1.99, 2.01)`` and ``(1.95, 2.01)``.
* **Scale and conditioning:** grids of 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, and 256 interior points;
  fixed modes 1 through 32 as declared by each sweep.  Log-ratio order estimates are
  sensitive to subtraction and division of successive small errors.
* **Oracle:** analytic discrete and continuum spectra, the closed-form sine dispersion
  relation, direct residuals, and independent log-ratio order calculations.
* **Rationale:** epsilon-scaled per-value allowances follow spacing and energy
  amplification; ``2e-10`` covers the more cancellation-sensitive derived order.
  The wider order windows test expected second-order trend rather than binary64
  equality.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept for fixed-mode finite
  convergence behavior only.  Uniform spectral convergence and continuum error for
  increasing mode index are explicitly unclaimed.

``TOL-15`` — Particle-in-a-box norm sweep
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** raw and relative norm comparisons use ``rtol = 2e-13`` with
  ``atol`` from ``2e-13`` to ``2e-11``; exact-zero compression remains exact;
  relative algebraic eigenpair residuals must be below ``1e-13``.
* **Scale and conditioning:** the same six grid sizes produce Hamiltonian and residual
  norms that grow with discretization dimension and spacing; Frobenius and spectral
  norms use reductions and eigensolver values at those scales.
* **Oracle:** closed-form tridiagonal eigenvalues and independently derived maximum,
  Frobenius, spectral, boundary, and discarded-sector norms.
* **Rationale:** relative terms track growing raw norms, while the absolute floors
  cover reduction and eigensolver roundoff near small residuals.  Exact structural
  zeros and monotonic trends are not tolerance-masked.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept for the represented
  finite matrices only.  Raw norms are basis- and discretization-dependent and are not
  continuum convergence metrics.

``TOL-16`` — Particle-in-a-box identifiability reconstruction
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Value and units:** decomposition reconstruction uses ``atol = 2e-14`` and
  ``rtol = 0``; independently recomputed Frobenius norms use ``atol = 2e-15`` and
  ``rtol = 0`` in the dimensionless retained representation.
* **Scale and conditioning:** ``3 x 3`` real symmetric matrices and short Frobenius
  reductions.
* **Oracle:** direct matrix addition, exact model-class projections, and independent
  Frobenius norms.
* **Rationale:** the small absolute envelopes cover only final-place matrix addition
  and reduction differences; best-fit and residual arrays, symmetry, ordering, and
  exact zero remain exact.
* **Scientific limitation and proposed disposition:** accept for algebraic
  reconstructability of this illustrative decomposition only.  It does not establish
  a physical potential or scientific identifiability.

Items outside this adequacy decision
------------------------------------

The following do not silently inherit approval from this register:

* exact comparisons, zero-tolerance branches, hashes, schemas, array identities,
  units, shapes, ordering, and unavailable-channel inventories;
* caller-selected tolerances stored and round-tripped as data when a test verifies
  only type, serialization, rejection, or inclusive-boundary semantics;
* Materials Project/pymatgen symmetry tolerances, Quantum ESPRESSO duality-policy
  values, and other provisional integrations for which ``v0.1.0`` makes no production
  or scientific adequacy claim;
* convergence parameters or acceptance thresholds in excluded production,
  bulk-silicon, external-tool, MPI, scheduler, or HPC work; and
* tolerances in uncollected research scripts or exploratory documents.

If a later release promotes one of these items into a supported numerical claim, it
requires its own value, unit, scale, conditioning, oracle, and limitation review.

Human decision
--------------

The human decision accepts ``TOL-01`` through ``TOL-16`` as adequate for the narrow
software and finite numerical claims stated here, with every listed scientific
limitation retained.  It does not assert that any value is suitable for material
prediction, production convergence, uncertainty quantification, or a different model,
mesh, basis, backend, platform, or dependency environment.

Any change to a registered value, comparison formula, oracle, represented scale, or
availability rule after approval reopens this review.  Human approval of this register
clears only the tolerance-adequacy portion of BLK-07; all other release gates remain
independent.

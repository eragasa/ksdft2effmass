# Required review passes

Every substantive packet receives all applicable passes. A campaign may assign
one pass to multiple humans or several passes to one human, but records the
assignment and any loss of independence.

## Mathematical and logical pass

Review:

- definitions before use;
- domains, codomains, state spaces, and quantifiers;
- assumptions and exceptional cases;
- derivations, proofs, estimates, and counterexamples;
- basis, gauge, unit, energy-reference, and geometry consistency;
- distinctions among projection, disentanglement, basis transformation,
  downfolding, block decoupling, and truncation; and
- distinctions among decay, locality, and exact finite support.

## Scientific and literature pass

Review:

- physical interpretation and assumptions;
- exact support supplied by each citation;
- source quality and bibliographic identity;
- textbook fact, literature result, project result, and proposal boundaries;
- novelty and contribution language; and
- synthetic, illustrative, calculated, reference-data, and material-result
  distinctions.

## Evidence and reproducibility pass

Review:

- provenance of every reported numerical result;
- training, withheld, locality, sensitivity, and certificate roles;
- exact formulas, thresholds, normalizations, units, and tolerances;
- producer and independent-verifier separation;
- source-byte replay limitations;
- negative evidence and unresolved outcomes;
- current versus proposed software capability; and
- whether failure to find a witness is incorrectly described as infeasibility.

## Pedagogy and editorial pass

Review:

- suitability for the declared audience;
- motivation before abstraction;
- symbolic reasoning before code where promised;
- continuity, pacing, examples, exercises, and chapter transitions;
- duplication that should be removed or replaced with a cross-reference;
- notation load and local reminders; and
- whether limitations are visible where claims are made.

Correctness and evidence review apply to technical appendices even when the
campaign assigns them a different narrative-polish standard from the core text.

## Mechanical pass

Run the publication's declared deterministic checks, including as applicable:

- source-graph and reference audits;
- citation-key and bibliography checks;
- style, format, and link checks;
- a clean document build;
- warnings, unresolved references, unresolved citations, and overfull boxes;
- figure, table, and retained-artifact existence; and
- source and output digests.

Mechanical success establishes only the behavior named by those checks. It does
not satisfy a human pass or establish scientific correctness.

## Pass record

Each pass records:

- exact campaign candidate and packet;
- assigned reviewer and role;
- coverage and completion date;
- evidence inspected;
- finding identifiers;
- unavailable evidence or qualification limitations; and
- whether a revision requires renewed review.

A pass without required human coverage remains incomplete even when an agent
found no problem.

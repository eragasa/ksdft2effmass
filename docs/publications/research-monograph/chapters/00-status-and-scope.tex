\chapter{Status, Scope, and Reading Contract}

\textbf{Status: proposed work and placeholders.}

This monograph separates physical models, mathematical operators, finite matrix
representations, software implementations, and retained evidence. It introduces
no execution authority and makes no claim that a planned calculation, proof,
comparison, or reduction has been completed. This chapter will maintain the
scope, notation map, evidence labels, and relationship to the project's owning
specifications and publication records.

\section{Software realization of scientific retention}
\index{software!scientific retention}
\index{retained operator!software representation}

\evidenceclass{Implemented software structure with synthetic software-verification
coverage; not numerical verification, scientific validation, uncertainty
quantification, release status, or proof completion.}

The current Python reference source under \texttt{ksdft2effmass.periodic}
implements the structural separation used in
Chapter~\ref{ch:representations-and-alignment} and
Appendix~\ref{app:operator-spaces}. The mapping is:

\begin{description}
  \item[\texttt{PeriodicOperatorReference}.] Stable parent-model,
    parent-operator, ambient-space, and spatial-dimension identities.
  \item[\texttt{PeriodicRetentionDefinition}.] Parent-qualified retention
    kind, rank, ordered retained labels, reciprocal domain, assumptions,
    construction record, and provenance.
  \item[\texttt{PeriodicRetainedSubspace}.] Retained mathematical-space
    identity and its ambient, projector-or-frame, spin, internal-degree,
    boundary, and provenance metadata.
  \item[\texttt{PeriodicRetainedOperator}.] Exact operator identity with the
    retained space as domain and codomain, plus construction,
    energy-reference, Hermiticity-declaration, and provenance metadata.
  \item[\texttt{PeriodicRepresentedRetainedOperator}.] Binding between the
    exact retained operator and one finite \texttt{OperatorRecord}, including
    representation-map and gauge identities.
\end{description}

The parent reference deliberately stores stable identities rather than an
arbitrary concrete model implementation. It is not a filesystem path,
repository root, remote identifier, or loader, and it does not prove that a
referenced artifact exists. Resolution and I/O remain separate operations.
Likewise, finite geometry, ordered basis, matrix entries, energy metadata, and
represented provenance remain owned by \texttt{OperatorRecord} rather than
being copied into a second operator container.

Three explicit construction Actions bind the layers: retained-subspace,
retained-operator, and represented-retained-operator constructors. Their
software contract rejects disagreement in parent identity, ambient and retained
dimensions, state-space identity, basis ordering, energy unit, and scalar energy
zero. It does not reorder states, infer a gauge, convert units, align energy
references, or repair an incompatible representation.

This implementation does not select eigenvectors, compute projectors, perform
Wannier localization, execute Fourier transformation, truncate hoppings, fit an
effective model, or run an electronic-structure calculation. Existing
numerical records remain distinct and require later specialized composition.
Consequently, the implementation realizes the manuscript's object boundaries
without supplying evidence that a particular retained space or reduced model is
scientifically adequate.

\section{Novelty and evidence boundary}
\index{proof!evidence boundary}


The finite-dimensional projector, compression, pullback, subtraction, and norm
identities are standard linear algebra. The project does not claim those
identities as new mathematics. A possible contribution is their
application-specific organization into one prover-neutral contract set,
independent Lean--Isabelle--Rocq encodings, semantic conformance review, dual
common-space impurity extraction, and composition with later locality,
continuum, and compatibility arguments. Priority over prior formalization has
not been established.

Potentially substantive mathematical results lie in the assumptions and bounds
that connect the elementary layer to the physical reduction: existence and
regularity of an adequate identification, gauge-constrained locality,
quantitative excluded-space control, well-posed crossover criteria,
operator-to-observable bounds, certified model-class incompatibility, and
atomistic-to-continuum consistency. Every such result remains proposed.

Proof establishes only the stated conclusion from the stated assumptions.
Numerical verification must still test implementations and bound usefulness;
scientific validation must still test the physical model for a declared use;
and human review must still decide whether the assumptions and interpretation
are acceptable.

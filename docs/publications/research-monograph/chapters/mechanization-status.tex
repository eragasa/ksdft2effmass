proposition actually relied upon.

Six questions must therefore remain separate: whether a proof exists;
whether its exact proposition and conventions match the manuscript;
whether KSD has admitted that encoding for its frozen contract; whether
independent backends and semantic review meet completion criteria; whether
runtime arrays and procedures are authenticated against the exact objects;
and whether the physical model is validated for a declared use. A positive
answer to one is not a positive answer to the others. In particular,
\texttt{unencoded} in the table above does not mean that no supporting Lean
algebra exists, and a supporting theorem does not change that table.

bounded exposition for several additional source families. Neither addition
asserts exhaustive declaration-level coverage, fresh Lean compilation,

\subsection{Source-neutral conjugate-linear coordinate algebra}
\label{sec:mechanization-conjugate-linear-coordinate-algebra}
\index{mechanized proof!conjugate-linear coordinates}

Separate external Lean evidence concerns a small algebraic slice of
Chapter~\ref{ch:symmetry-actions-represented-subspaces}, not the frozen
\texttt{PRF-05} contracts above. The owning repository is
\texttt{projectkoios-formal}; its module is
\path{ProjectKoios/BandGauge/Alignment/ConjugateLinearCoordinates.lean}.
The declaration manifest owns definitions \texttt{BG-ALIGNMENT-DEF-050}
through \texttt{056} and supporting lemmas \texttt{BG-ALIGNMENT-LEM-012}
through \texttt{019}. Three named lemmas supply the relevant conclusions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{BG-ALIGNMENT-LEM-014} proves the basis-change action
    with $M'=A_{\mathrm t}^{-1}M\overline{A_{\mathrm s}}$. Here $M$ is a
    rectangular complex matrix acting on conjugated source coefficients,
    $A_{\mathrm s}$ and $A_{\mathrm t}$ are independently invertible source
    and target basis matrices, and the overline is entrywise complex
    conjugation. Source and target finite dimensions may differ.
  \item \texttt{BG-ALIGNMENT-LEM-018} proves that the algebraic equation
    $M^{\dagger}G_{\mathrm t}M=\overline{G_{\mathrm s}}$ is preserved under
    that coordinate change and $G'_a=A_a^{\dagger}G_aA_a$. In this statement
    $G_{\mathrm s}$ and $G_{\mathrm t}$ are arbitrary square complex
    matrices of compatible sizes, not already qualified inner-product metrics;
    the dagger is conjugate transpose and $a$ denotes either space.
  \item \texttt{BG-ALIGNMENT-LEM-019} proves that composing coordinate
    actions $x\mapsto N\overline x$ and $y\mapsto M\overline y$ gives
    $x\mapsto M\overline N x$. The source vector $x$, intermediate vector
    $y$ and matrices have explicitly compatible source, middle and target
    coordinate types.
\end{itemize}
The exact names and hypotheses are retained in
\path{declaration-signatures.log}; the module's formal manifest and provisional
source crosswalk distinguish definitions from proved supporting lemmas.

\section{Novelty and evidence boundary}
\index{proof!evidence boundary}


The finite-dimensional projector, compression, pullback, subtraction, and norm
identities are standard linear algebra. The project does not claim those
identities as new mathematics. A possible contribution is their
application-specific organization into one prover-neutral contract set,
independent Lean--Isabelle--Rocq encodings, semantic conformance review, dual
common-space impurity extraction, and composition with later locality,
continuum, and compatibility arguments. Priority over prior formalization has
not been established.

Potentially substantive mathematical results lie in the assumptions and bounds
that connect the elementary layer to the physical reduction: existence and
regularity of an adequate identification, gauge-constrained locality,
quantitative excluded-space control, well-posed crossover criteria,
operator-to-observable bounds, certified model-class incompatibility, and
atomistic-to-continuum consistency. Every such result remains proposed.

Proof establishes only the stated conclusion from the stated assumptions.
Numerical verification must still test implementations and bound usefulness;
scientific validation must still test the physical model for a declared use;
and human review must still decide whether the assumptions and interpretation
are acceptable.

\chapter{First-Principles Parent Model}
\label{ch:first-principles-parent}

\textbf{Status: accepted model and numerical protocols are described; no
production calculation or converged value is claimed.}

This chapter describes how the pristine-silicon Kohn--Sham parent is to be
constructed and recorded. The physical branch is owned by
\texttt{specification/ksdft2Effmass.physical-specification.v1.md}; the numerical
protocol is owned by
\texttt{specification/ksdft2Effmass.numerical-specification.v1.md}. The present
account does not replace either specification.

\section{Declared bulk branch}

The immediate parent problem is pristine silicon in the diamond structure,
represented by a two-atom primitive cell for primary bulk calculations. The
accepted branch uses PBE, a scalar-relativistic PseudoDojo optimized
norm-conserving Vanderbilt silicon pseudopotential, no spin--orbit coupling, and
no spin polarization. The production lattice parameter is an output of a
zero-pressure PBE optimization using the selected pseudopotential; it is not
inserted from experiment by convention.

This branch defines a Kohn--Sham reference. Its known physical limitations and
any comparison with experiment are parent-model issues. They remain distinct
from convergence of the numerical representation and from later errors caused
by projection or model reduction.

\section{External-program boundary}

Quantum ESPRESSO is responsible for self-consistent, non-self-consistent,
band-path, and lattice-optimization calculations. The project does not
reimplement plane-wave Kohn--Sham DFT. For the declared workflow,
\texttt{pw.x} is the relevant executable for the bulk electronic-structure
stages. Wannier construction and the QE--Wannier interface are addressed in
Chapter~\ref{ch:representations-and-alignment}; they remain responsibilities of
Wannier90 and \texttt{pw2wannier90.x}, respectively.

The software in this repository begins at explicit integration and provenance
boundaries. It may prepare or validate declared inputs, record execution
identity, parse retained outputs, and construct downstream representations. A
successful process exit does not establish convergence, correctness of the
physical branch, or scientific acceptance.

No production executable is invoked in developing this monograph. Production
QE and Wannier90 execution is a separately authorized activity with declared
inputs, resources, outputs, and retention policy.

\section{Pseudopotential identity}

The numerical specification records the approved silicon pseudopotential by
source table, release, filename, relativistic form, valence count, cutoff hints,
and SHA-256 hashes of both compressed and decompressed files. This identity is
part of the physical and numerical parent. A filename alone is insufficient,
and a later download is not assumed identical merely because its URL or element
label matches.

The PseudoDojo hints are starting information for convergence, not accepted
production cutoffs. They are expressed in Hartree, whereas Quantum ESPRESSO
uses Rydberg for \texttt{ecutwfc}; the conversion and the units of every
recorded setting must therefore be explicit. Phosphorus and boron
pseudopotentials are not selected by the bulk protocol and belong to their
later specialization gates.

\section{Controlled convergence}

Numerical convergence is evaluated by changing one controlled variable at a
time while holding the physical branch and the remaining numerical settings
fixed. A parameter is accepted only when the declared target observables meet
the applicable stability criteria and the retained record identifies both
accepted and rejected candidates.

\subsection{Plane-wave and charge-density cutoffs}

The cutoff study begins from a monotone sequence that brackets and extends the
approved pseudopotential hints. For the norm-conserving silicon potential, the
first charge-density cutoff trial is tied explicitly to the wavefunction cutoff;
a separate ratio refinement is required if charge-density sensitivity controls
the result. The smallest pair meeting every declared tolerance is selected,
with a higher guard setting retained when feasible.

A total-energy plateau alone is not sufficient when a stricter setting is
required by the indirect gap, valley position, effective masses, stress, or
another declared parent observable. Conversely, a higher cutoff is not adopted
merely because it is more expensive or appears more conservative.

\subsection{Brillouin-zone sampling}

Four sampling purposes remain separate:

\begin{description}
  \item[SCF mesh.] A uniform Monkhorst--Pack sequence converges the charge
    density and total energy of the primitive bulk cell.
  \item[Band path.] An explicitly recorded high-symmetry path reports the band
    structure and supplies declared withheld points.
  \item[Valley sampling.] A local one-dimensional refinement locates the
    conduction minimum along the selected $\Gamma$--$X$-type direction.
  \item[Effective-mass sampling.] Local stencils around the selected conduction
    minimum estimate longitudinal and transverse curvature.
\end{description}

A dense path is not a substitute for an SCF integration mesh. The valley and
mass calculations use the accepted self-consistent density but require their
own coordinates, refinement intervals, fit or finite-difference rules, and
stability checks.

\section{Equilibrium lattice procedure}

The production lattice parameter is determined from a symmetric set of
compressed and expanded primitive cells. Every point uses identical numerical
settings and retains energy and stress conventions. An equation of state or a
stated local polynomial is fit near the minimum, with included points, excluded
points, residuals, and uncertainty recorded. Refinement continues according to
the accepted numerical criterion, followed by a calculation or constrained
relaxation that checks the selected minimum.

The resulting lattice parameter is then frozen for compatible bulk and dopant
lattice vectors. An experimental lattice parameter may be evaluated as an
external validation branch, but it does not silently replace the internally
consistent PBE parent geometry.

\section{Target parent quantities}

The bulk parent record eventually supplies

\begin{itemize}
  \item the equilibrium lattice parameter and its numerical uncertainty;
  \item the indirect Kohn--Sham gap relative to the declared band-edge
    convention;
  \item the conduction-valley position in an explicitly stated reciprocal or
    path coordinate;
  \item longitudinal and transverse electron effective masses, including fit
    axes and stencil stability; and
  \item selected band energies at points withheld from later fitting.
\end{itemize}

These are future calculated results. The accepted numerical specification fixes
how stability is judged but deliberately contains no converged cutoff, mesh,
lattice parameter, gap, valley coordinate, or mass. Values cannot be promoted
into this chapter until their calculation identity and retained provenance are
available.

\section{Provenance record}

A reproducible parent calculation requires more than its input text. The compact
record must identify, as applicable,

\begin{itemize}
  \item repository revision and immutable input identities;
  \item executable path, version, and build or environment provenance;
  \item pseudopotential source, release, checksums, and recorded license;
  \item physical branch, cell vectors, positions, units, and symmetry choices;
  \item cutoff, mesh, occupation, solver, and convergence settings;
  \item MPI and OpenMP configuration and the exact command;
  \item expected and observed output inventory; and
  \item checksums and locations for externally retained artifacts.
\end{itemize}

Large wavefunctions, densities, restart state, scratch directories, and dense
native outputs remain outside Git. Compact sanitized inputs, manifests,
checksums, software identities, and calculation-status summaries may be retained
when authorized.

\section{Tutorial and production evidence}

The repository also defines an inactive Quantum ESPRESSO tutorial campaign.
Those exercises are intended to learn executable behavior, file boundaries,
stream capture, failure modes, and provenance mechanics. Even if executed later,
tutorial observations remain software-workflow evidence. They do not become the
production silicon parent, establish numerical convergence, or validate the
scientific model.

This distinction allows operational learning to proceed without contaminating
the evidentiary status of the research calculation. The parent model becomes a
calculated project result only through its own authorized execution,
convergence record, and scientific interpretation.

\section{Aligned impurity extraction}
\index{alignment!impurity extraction}
\index{operator difference!impurity}


For dopant $d\in\{\mathrm P,\mathrm B\}$, let
$\overline H_{\mathrm W,d}$ be the doped Wannier operator after scalar-energy
alignment, let $\overline H_{\mathrm W,b}$ be the matched pristine bulk
operator in the corresponding supercell convention, and let $U_d$ identify the
bulk coordinates with the doped comparison space. The planned signed impurity
operator is
\begin{equation}
  \Delta H_{\mathrm W,d}
  =
  \overline H_{\mathrm W,d}
  -
  U_d\overline H_{\mathrm W,b}U_d^{\dagger}.
\end{equation}
This expression is meaningful only with the recorded basis, geometry, site
mapping, energy reference, spin convention, and alignment direction. For boron,
the final branch must retain the accepted spinor and spin--orbit structure. For
phosphorus, the neutral spin-polarized branch must not be interpreted silently
as an ionized donor-core operator.

The operator is computed over a supercell sequence. Stability of its spatial
blocks, gauge and alignment sensitivity, and periodic-image contamination are
required before a dopant-specific operator can be frozen. One finite-cell
difference is an impurity-operator candidate, not an isolated-impurity result.

\begin{abstract}
This document is a long-form research-writing workspace for the construction,
comparison, and reduction of semiconductor Hamiltonians from first-principles
Kohn--Sham calculations. It is a prospective research monograph, not a completed
dissertation or validated scientific result. Each substantive claim remains
subject to its owning specification, provenance, verification, validation, and
human-acceptance records.
\end{abstract}

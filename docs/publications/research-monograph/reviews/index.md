# Research-monograph review campaigns

## Purpose

This directory retains human-in-the-loop review campaigns for the research
monograph. Each campaign binds one version of the reusable
[publication-review protocol](../../review-protocol/README.md) to exact source
and rendered candidates.

Campaign records are review provenance. They do not replace the monograph,
scientific specifications, retained calculations, proof packages, or software
contracts. Campaign completion does not by itself establish scientific
validation, publication acceptance, submission, or release.

| [`RM-REVIEW-001`](RM-REVIEW-001/README.md) | `PLANNING` | proposed `v0.1` | not frozen | First packetized human-in-the-loop review of the long-form monograph |

## Retention rule

A closed campaign is not rewritten to cover later manuscript bytes. A new
campaign records supersession and identifies the previous campaign. Planning
records must not be described as completed review.

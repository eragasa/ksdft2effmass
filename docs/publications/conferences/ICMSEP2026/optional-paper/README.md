This directory contains the earlier combined source-first draft associated with the
accepted ICMSEP 2026 abstract:

> *Compatibility of spectral and operator reductions of a first-principles silicon
> Hamiltonian*

## Status

The manuscript is a **pre-results methodology draft**, not a submission-ready paper.
Its main text presents the general compatibility framework and bounded decision logic.
The planned silicon execution protocol, pending result tables, and non-production
software bootstrap are isolated in appendices until authenticated production results
exist. The draft must not be represented as demonstrating a validated silicon
Hamiltonian, a completed spectral/operator compatibility test, or an accepted
tight-binding model.

The existing `../manuscript/` package remains the longer Computational Materials
Science draft. This directory is a separate conference-linked paper draft and does
not replace it.

## Format basis

No distinct ICMSEP full-paper template was available from the public conference page
when this package was started. The draft therefore follows the publicly available
[Philippine Engineering Journal author guidelines](https://nec.up.edu.ph/authors-guide/),
retrieved 2026-10-10, because ICMSEP announced a route by which recommended papers may
be considered by that journal. The target must be confirmed with the conference
organizers before formatting or submission.

The PEJ guidance currently calls for:

- an editable A4 Word document and a reference PDF;
- at most 30 pages for a technical paper;
- 12-point Times New Roman, double-spaced main text;
- an abstract of at most 300 words in 10-point italic text;
- five alphabetized keywords;
- Roman-numeral main headings and Arabic-numbered subheadings;
- numbered equations, figures, tables, and citation-order references; and
- Introduction, Methodology, Results and Discussion, Conclusions and
  Recommendations, Acknowledgments, References, and Nomenclature sections.

`manuscript.md` is the maintained prose source while scientific results are still
incomplete. A `.docx` should be generated or formatted only after the organizers
confirm the target and template. No journal target has been selected; journal fit and
submission requirements must be checked against current official guidance before any
reformatting or submission.

## Before submission

1. Confirm whether the optional paper is a conference proceeding, a PEJ referral, or
   both, and obtain the exact template and deadline.
2. Populate the appendix evidence records with authenticated retained results, then
   promote only supported findings into Section III.
3. Complete the production parent, Wannier, fitting, withheld-validation, and
   sensitivity calculations under their separate authorities.
4. Predeclare threshold-derivation rules before inspecting production evidence; after
   measuring the parent numerical and extraction floors, freeze the resulting numerical
   thresholds before any compatibility fit or admissible-set search.
5. Add final figures and editable tables with reconstructable source data.
6. Confirm with the conference or target journal that a sole-author optional paper is
   permitted for the accepted abstract, and verify authorship, acknowledgments,
   funding, conflicts, and the AI-assistance declaration.
7. Convert to the required editable format and validate page count, fonts, margins,
   equations, references, and artwork.
8. Obtain explicit human authorization before submitting or publishing anything.

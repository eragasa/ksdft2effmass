# ICMSEP 2026 conference-paper split

The former combined optional-paper narrative has been separated into two manuscripts:

1. [`conference-paper-1/manuscript.md`](conference-paper-1/manuscript.md) owns the
   material-neutral method and controlled 1D/2D numerical-verification results.
2. [`conference-paper-2/manuscript.md`](conference-paper-2/manuscript.md) owns the
   first-principles bulk-silicon application and remains explicitly pre-results.

Each paper stores its appendices under its own `appendices/` directory. These files own
the evidence-curation and future-work plans; the manuscripts link to them rather than
duplicating their contents.

The accepted ICMSEP abstract is closest in title and application scope to conference
paper 2. Conference paper 1 supplies the independently bounded methodological and
controlled-model foundation. Whether the conference permits two papers, or requires one
paper tied to the accepted abstract, must be confirmed before submission.

The existing `optional-paper/` directory is retained as development history and is not
the authoritative split manuscript.

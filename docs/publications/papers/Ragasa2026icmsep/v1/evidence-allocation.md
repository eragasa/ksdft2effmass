# Evidence allocation for the ICMSEP synthesis

## Relationship to existing manuscripts

This synthesis **accompanies** rather than supersedes the existing focused manuscripts.

| Surface | Retained role | What this synthesis does not do |
|---|---|---|
| PIAB1D and QHO1D calibration reports | Separately retained spectral-reduction and finite-spatial-representation studies | Uses their conceptual error hierarchy without converting their development observations into silicon evidence |
| `Ragasa2026a` | Focused exact rank-two theory and controlled numerical calculation | Does not replace its theorem statements, claim inventory, or evidence lifecycle |
| `Ragasa2026d` | Provenance-oriented silicon reduction plan/status record | Does not rewrite its historical sequence or convert planned work retroactively into completed evidence |
| `Ragasa2026icmsep` | Long-form cross-scale synthesis using completed calculations | Does not create new calculation authority or publication qualification |
| Seven-paper roadmap | Planning/allocation surface | Is not silently treated as an eighth independently evidenced research result |

## Allocation rule

The synthesis may reproduce or summarize evidence owned elsewhere, but every numerical claim remains owned by its canonical calculation directory. The synthesis owns only the cross-scale narrative and its explicit claim boundaries.

The manuscript therefore uses four labels:

1. **analytic result** — proved under stated mathematical assumptions;
2. **controlled development calculation** — sampled synthetic evidence with its existing lifecycle status;
3. **producer-checked material-derived development result** — authenticated source and outputs, but no independent reconstruction;
4. **external adequacy audit** — DFT-parent comparison kept separate from source-relative reduction.

## Non-duplication decision

No roadmap or experiment-allocation document is changed in v1. A later human review should decide whether the synthesis is:

- a conference-only integrative presentation that leaves the seven-paper journal allocation unchanged;
- an umbrella review/overview manuscript that cites Papers A and D after their publication; or
- a replacement for one planned roadmap item.

Until that decision is made, the synthesis must not claim exclusive allocation of evidence already assigned to Papers A, D, or later planned studies.

## Authorship and submission

The displayed author line follows the current working sources and is provisional. Final authorship, acknowledgments, conference format, length, and submission destination require human review and explicit authorization.

# Paper 1 v3 successor-commitment crosswalk for Paper 2

## Purpose

Paper 1 v3 presents a two-axis validation ladder. Paper 2 owns the next **spatial and
lattice** rungs while deliberately retaining rank two; Paper 3 owns higher-rank/witness
work and Paper 4 owns the first-principles silicon endpoint. This ledger prevents Paper 2
from omitting a promised spatial-lattice control, importing a different paper's scope, or
presenting development diagnostics as qualified evidence.

This is an editorial crosswalk, not a scientific authority or evidence package. Paper 1
v3 definitions remain in its manuscript and supplement; Paper 2 results remain owned by
the publication-independent experiment families.

## Status vocabulary

- **Delivered analytically:** Paper 2 contains the applicable proof or definition.
- **Implemented in development:** software and bounded records exist, without qualified
  publication-evidence status.
- **Open for qualification:** needed before Paper 2 can make the corresponding numerical
  claim.
- **Outside Paper 2:** intentionally owned by Paper 3, Paper 4, or later work.

## E. Crosswalk conclusion

Paper 2 contains every **rank-two spatial/lattice family** that Paper 1 v3 sold, but
family presence is not calculation parity. The maintained 1-D diagnostic catalog is now
the mandatory row-level predecessor: every applicable quantity must be recalculated in
each 2-D/3-D family, and every inapplicable quantity must carry an explicit reason.

The first strict audit exposed a substantive gap: the bounded Paper 2 calculator had
reported a best global rotation at fixed unit splitting scale, whereas Paper 1's minimum
operator threshold additionally optimizes the positive splitting scale and separately
reports the unit-scale exact-spectrum threshold. That distinction is now explicit in the
parity contract and calculator implementation. All four three-level artifact ladders
have been regenerated, and every catalog row now has an implemented, explicitly
unresolved or explicitly inapplicable mapping. The two ordered closure phases now use
the worst-case magnitude over reciprocal direction and transverse base point. An
independent explicit finite-sum oracle reconstructs every applicable calculated row in
2-D and 3-D, closing software-level row parity without establishing scientific
acceptance, formal convergence, or retained evidence.

The other blockers remain frozen caps and tolerances, strain refinement and frozen
resource gates, pointwise/global strain comparison, retained
provenance, and authorized confirmatory execution. Anisotropic physical laws,
higher-rank gauge classes, and material targets remain correctly outside Paper 2.

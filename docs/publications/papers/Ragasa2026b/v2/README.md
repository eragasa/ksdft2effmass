# Conference paper 2, editorial revision 2

This directory contains the publication-structured revision of Paper 2. It preserves the
mathematical statements, numerical values, and scientific scope of
[`../v1/`](../v1/) while presenting the work in the
standard sequence: Introduction, Theoretical Framework and Methods, Results, Discussion,
Conclusions, and Appendices.

The revision uses declarative scientific language and moves software-oriented validation
detail to the appendices. Numerical results are reported as calculated outcomes of the
declared finite synthetic models. Their scope remains explicit: finite meshes and finite
strain samples do not establish continuum extrema, and graphene-/silicon-inspired
controls are not material parametrizations.

Figures remain owned and generated by the Paper 2 experiment compositions. The TeX
source resolves them through the sibling `v1/figures/` directory rather
than duplicating generated files. Those rendered assets are ignored development outputs
and must be regenerated using the `v1/figures/README.md` commands before a clean-worktree
normal build; draft-graphics source validation does not replace that step. The discussion
now records the current maintained
experiment succession: four verified but replaceable dimensional development ladders,
two bounded M0/M1/M2 strain families, and the unexecuted isotropic/anisotropic support-
hierarchy sequence. Only the bounded calculations are reported as development results;
prospective hierarchy designs supply no numerical evidence. Build locally with:

```console
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error manuscript.tex
```

The program-wide [allocation ledger](../../experiment-to-paper-allocation.md) binds the
dimensional, strain, cross-family composition, and support-hierarchy experiment surfaces
to Paper 2 without transferring their independent identities.

This editorial revision does not authorize submission, publication, release, or a change
to the evidence status of the underlying calculations.

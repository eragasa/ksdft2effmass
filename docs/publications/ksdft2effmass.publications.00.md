# KSDFT to Effective-Mass Theory: Publications

## Purpose

This index points to publication planning, conference material, and manuscript
working files. A package-level `manuscript.md` is a living projection of work in
the repository: its prospective statements authorize the corresponding
in-repository work within its declared scope, and its links or proposed links
identify the intended owning surfaces. That authorization does not represent the
projected work as completed, does not establish submission, acceptance,
publication, scientific validation, or release status, and does not replace an
applicable checkpoint for protected external or production execution.

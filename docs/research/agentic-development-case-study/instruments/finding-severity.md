# Finding severity

| Severity | Definition |
|---|---|
| critical | Could invalidate scientific results or corrupt persistent data. |
| material | Violates an accepted contract or blocks integration. |
| minor | Local defect without contract-level or scientific consequences. |
| note | Improvement without a demonstrated failure. |

Severity records expected consequence at the time of review. A finding may be
reclassified only with evidence and, for scientific or architectural impact,
human review.

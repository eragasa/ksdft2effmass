## Result boundary

The reviewed literature establishes precedents for individual components:
finite-rank impurity resolvents, weak binding, periodic band-edge
homogenization, discrete-to-continuum convergence, periodic-volume effects,
semiconductor multivalley and central-cell modeling, localized-basis defect
operators, Wannier interpolation, and gauge-aligned model stitching. It does
not establish that this project's represented benchmark satisfies the
hypotheses of those results.

The defensible contribution statement is therefore limited to an integrated,
reproducible controlled exercise that keeps representation compatibility,
operator extraction, model-class residuals, route commutativity, finite-rank
spectral checks, finite-volume effects, and distinct continuum-refinement axes
in one fail-closed diagnostic chain. The review found close component-level
precedents and a recent preprint with explicit assignment and Procrustes
alignment of independently generated clean and defect Wannier models. It does
not support a claim that the project invented impurity resolvents, effective
mass theory, Wannier defect operators, gauge matching, continuum limits, or
finite-size analysis.

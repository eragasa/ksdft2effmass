# Scientific method

```math
G_{\mathrm{fcc}}=\frac{a_c^2}{4}
\begin{bmatrix}2&1&1\\1&2&1\\1&1&2\end{bmatrix}
```

with diamond basis positions `tau_A=(0,0,0)` and `tau_B=(1/4,1/4,1/4)`. Four
equal-length tetrahedral links connect the two basis sites through cell shifts
`{0,-e_1,-e_2,-e_3}`. Their normalized structure factor is

```math
\gamma_{\mathrm{dia}}(q)=\frac14
\left(1+e^{-2\pi i q_1}+e^{-2\pi i q_2}+e^{-2\pi i q_3}\right).
```

A two-site, two-channel four-state parent supplies a gapped lower rank-two group. This is
synthetic structural inspiration only: it is not an `sp3s*` model, silicon
parametrization, valley model, effective-mass result, or material claim.

A separate matched simple-cubic/FCC Bravais control uses one Hermitian orbital bond
matrix. Integer relations among its twelve-bond FCC orbit produce a constrained
three-index Bessel comparator. The primary inspired and control fields are separately
rotated by the applicable Bravais-orbit gauge. Pointwise spectra and full covariant
operators agree before truncation; the scientific residual permits only one global
`SO(2)` rotation. Primary support uses complete cell blocks under the Bravais metric,
while a secondary diagnostic reports basis-aware entry distances.

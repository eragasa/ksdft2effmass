# Numerical boundary

Finite transforms use reduced reciprocal coordinates and integer direct-lattice
translations. Truncation inventories must be dense in attainable physical metric shells,
complete under the exact integer point group, nested, and conjugate closed.

Construction meshes, shifted evaluation meshes, cyclic Nyquist conversion, deliberate
truncation, and total consequences remain separate. A unimodular primitive-basis control
must compare physical objects only after exact coordinate relabeling. Meshes, caps,
tolerances, resource limits, and convergence outcomes are experiment-specific.

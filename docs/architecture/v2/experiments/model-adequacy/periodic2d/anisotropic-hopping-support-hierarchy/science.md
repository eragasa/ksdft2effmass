# Anisotropic 2-D scientific method

## Scientific delta

The experiment isolates anisotropy in the already exercised two-dimensional setting so
it is not introduced simultaneously with a third coordinate.

- Axis permutations are not assumed to be model symmetries.
- Directional nearest-neighbor coefficients may differ.
- Equal integer radius or physical length does not automatically identify coefficients.
- Sign or inversion symmetry is used only when the frozen parent admits it.

## Evidence interpretation

The experiment answers a controlled anisotropy question rather than validating a
material or a general lattice implementation.

- Compatibility requires a same-parameter spectral/operator witness.
- Separation requires a conservative lower bound above resolution.
- Evaluation and locality remain downstream diagnostics.
- Outcomes do not transfer automatically to anisotropic 3-D.

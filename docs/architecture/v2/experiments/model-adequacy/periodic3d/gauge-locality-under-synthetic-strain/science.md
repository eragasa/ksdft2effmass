body-centered-tetragonal primitive basis is

\[
A_3(r)=\begin{pmatrix}
a/2&-a/2&a/2\\
a/2&a/2&-a/2\\
c/2&c/2&c/2
\end{pmatrix},\qquad G_3(r)=A_3(r)^\mathsf{T}A_3(r),
\]

\((1/2,-1/4,1/4)_B\), and is a permutation modulo every finite periodic size. This
resolves the common-cell state-space identification without identifying the strained
operators themselves.

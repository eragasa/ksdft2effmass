# Periodic 3-D scientific method

## Three-dimensional representation

The package studies scalar finite-periodic parents indexed by three reduced reciprocal
coordinates and three integer lattice coordinates.

- Direct and reciprocal bases are recorded explicitly.
- A finite represented hopping family is distinguished from an infinite-lattice model.
- Hermitian closure pairs each integer representative with its negative.
- Point-group equivalence is verified for the declared parent rather than inferred from dimension.

## Isotropy and anisotropy

The two children isolate cubic axis-permutation symmetry as a scientific control.

- The isotropic child preserves signed coordinate permutations admitted by a simple-cubic parent.
- The anisotropic child preserves sign symmetry but removes axis permutations.
- `Isotropic` does not mean continuous rotational invariance.
- `Anisotropic` does not imply a particular material crystal class.

## Compared evidence

Both children compare spectral and represented-operator adequacy in one maximal
coefficient space.

- Spectral observations use a frozen three-dimensional reciprocal sample.
- Operator observations use a frozen complete represented block domain.
- Support classes remain explicit experiment choices.
- Evaluation and locality observations remain downstream of decisive evidence.

## Sources and references

This package page summarizes project-defined synthetic constructions rather than
claiming a literature-owned material model.

- The isotropic child must cite its exact protocol when authored.
- The anisotropic child cites its maintained experiment protocol and threshold-design record.
- Reusable lattice conventions are delegated to reviewed PhysKit contracts.
- Literature references belong at the narrowest module or class that implements a sourced method.

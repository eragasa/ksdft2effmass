# Scientific interpretation

The object identifies the finite represented vector space in which matrix entries are
interpreted. State-space identity, ordered basis, geometry, energy zero and unit,
mathematical scalar field, and field-coordinate convention remain explicit and distinct.

Equal dimension does not imply equal space or basis. The scalar-field identity is
mathematical metadata and is not a NumPy dtype. Construction does not prove basis
completeness, orthonormality, physical equivalence, unit compatibility, or suitability of
the stated geometry.

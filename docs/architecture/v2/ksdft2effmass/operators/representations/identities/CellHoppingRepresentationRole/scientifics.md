# Scientific interpretation

`CellHoppingRepresentationRole.complete_declared_inventory` means that the coefficient
field covers every member of its declared finite Fourier inventory. It never means that
an infinite lattice or continuum representation is complete.

`CellHoppingRepresentationRole.support_truncated` means that the coefficient field is
restricted to declared support. The support definition, metric, closure properties, and
qualification evidence belong to later support and evidence owners.

Finite Fourier conversion and support truncation are derivation edges, not additional
members of this representation-role enum.

## Boundary

Decoding belongs to serialization owners; scientific construction belongs to explicit
campaign Actions and result owners; repository location and evidence correlation belong
to operation boundaries. No meaning is inferred from filenames, paths, hashes, shapes,
or field names by the encoded-document DataObject.

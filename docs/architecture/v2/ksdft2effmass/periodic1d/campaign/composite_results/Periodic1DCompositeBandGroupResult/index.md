# `Periodic1DCompositeBandGroupResult`

## Purpose and status

This implemented row-025 campaign result retains every typed evidence channel for one
historical composite band group without claiming to be a retained mathematical space or
exact retained operator.

## Channel ownership

- `wilson`: group identity, ordered parent-band inventory, unordered canonical phase
  spectrum, and retained controlled-gauge defect;
- `isolation`: sampled internal/external gaps and historical threshold disposition;
- `gauge_comparison`: overlap conditioning, covariance, alignment, eigenvalue, and
  unaligned hopping diagnostics;
- `hopping_representation`: available smooth reciprocal matrices and complete
  smooth/rough hopping families plus represented diagnostics;
- `range_study`: ordered smooth/rough finite-range outcomes;
- `direct_route`: direct-fit versus transform-and-truncate comparison; and
- `identities`: hashes of historical intermediate arrays.

Intrinsic checks enforce exact channel types, represented rank equal to selected band
count, a nonempty unique increasing range inventory, and inclusion of the direct-route
range.

## Scientific and unavailable-data boundary

Rank, band order, Wilson phases, or hashes alone do not define a retained space. Smooth
projector identity remains campaign evidence because projector coordinates are
unavailable. Source frame/projector arrays, attacked gauges, rough reciprocal matrices,
and withheld reciprocal matrices are not reconstructed.

## Evidence

The composite result serializer/verifier retains and independently reconstructs only the
available channels. `TestPeriodic1DCompositeScientificAdoption` proves that adoption
preserves the exact source object while constructing separate retained objects. Passing
establishes bounded retained-evidence and software behavior, not topology or material
claims, validation, UQ, or acceptance.

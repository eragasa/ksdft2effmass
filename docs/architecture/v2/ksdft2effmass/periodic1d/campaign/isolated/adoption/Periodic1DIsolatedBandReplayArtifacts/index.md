# `Periodic1DIsolatedBandReplayArtifacts`

## Purpose and status

This implemented row-023 retained-evidence aggregate preserves the exact authenticated
campaign objects, sidecar bytes, rank-one frame path, reconstructed-projector content
identity, complete hopping family, and ordered range-specific coefficient inventories.

## Intrinsic invariants

The experiment identity must correlate the exact definition and result objects supplied
to the decoder; same-identifier replacements are rejected. Sidecar bytes must match
their own SHA-256. The frame is rank one, the complete hopping model is scalar, and
range artifacts are exact typed, nonempty, unique, and increasing. Frame, projector,
and complete-coefficient identities use canonical lowercase SHA-256 syntax.

## Representation and provenance boundary

The frame bytes represent one gauge. The projector digest authenticates projectors
deterministically reconstructed as `u(k) u(k)^dagger`; dense projector bytes are not
duplicated. Neither digest defines the mathematical retained-space identity. Source
hashes establish observed-content correlation and race detection, not independent
historical provenance.

## Evidence and limitations

The retained sidecar, README, protocol, verifier, and claim-bearing adoption tests are
the evidence set. Passing does not establish localization-density reconstruction,
untruncated-parent convergence, material relevance, scientific validation, UQ, or
acceptance.

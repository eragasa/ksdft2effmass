# `Periodic1DReductionChallengeCampaignResult`

## Role

Frozen, slotted aggregate of the exact source document and all typed version-one
challenge observations. The source document remains a wire object; contained arrays
and diagnostics remain finite represented observations. Neither becomes a physical
parent model, retained subspace/operator, represented-operator identity, or effective
model by containment.

## Invariants

The aggregate requires exact child types, the historical `STRESS` wire discriminator,
and the exact historical evidence-status literal. Child Results enforce finite
binary64 values, exact scalar representations, nonempty ordered inventories,
nonnegative residuals, and canonical source-document consistency. The serializer
separately enforces exact root and nested version-one field sets before adaptation.

## Claim boundary

A constructed result establishes intrinsic consistency of decoded content only. It
does not establish that the maintained runner produced the bytes, that the result is
scientifically valid, or that the historical producer's documented expected trends
hold. Correlation and independent numerical verification are separate Actions.

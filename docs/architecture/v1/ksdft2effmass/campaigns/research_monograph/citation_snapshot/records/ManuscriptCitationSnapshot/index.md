# `ManuscriptCitationSnapshot`

Complete immutable owner snapshot for one exact HEAD-aligned manuscript graph and
bibliography. It contains fixed contract/parser/generator identities, source lineage,
all indexed structural record tuples, and exact closure sets. Construction succeeds
only after complete bounds, identity, relationship, locator, and closure replay. Its
`snapshot_id` covers revision and source identities; complete derived content is
additionally bound by the enclosing Result identity.

# Phase 1 scientific scope

Phase 1 distinguishes a mathematical operator from its finite representation and from the
history by which one representation is derived from another.

A representation identity states its finite carrier, represented space, basis, energy
reference, coordinate convention, intended role, and source identity. A transformation
identity states a directed source, target, kind, method, and orientation. Neither identity
contains evidence that the represented object or transformation is mathematically valid.

Phase 1 does not establish Hermiticity, projector identities, isometry, unitarity,
periodicity, sewing, isolation, support completeness, Fourier correctness, alignment
admissibility, model adequacy, or scientific acceptance.

# Scientific interpretation

A representation identity names one finite representation node. Its domain identifies the
carrier, its space identifies the interpreted finite vector space, its role describes the
node's intrinsic place within that carrier family, and its source artifact names the
originating formula or artifact.

Gauge conjugation, alignment, Fourier conversion, truncation, and reconstruction are not
node roles. They belong to directed transformation identities. Identity construction does
not bind matrix values or establish a mathematical property.

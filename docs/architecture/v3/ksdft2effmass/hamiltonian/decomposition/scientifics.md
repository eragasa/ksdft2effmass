# Scientific interpretation

Hamiltonian decomposition asks whether a represented Hamiltonian can be separated into
declared components under an explicit physical and mathematical interpretation. An
algebraically exact reconstruction does not by itself identify a unique physical
kinetic operator or potential.

For the PIAB1D first caller, the calculation demonstrates decomposition
nonuniqueness. The declared Dirichlet decomposition and an illustratively shifted
decomposition reconstruct the same retained Hamiltonian, while only the declared
Dirichlet decomposition carries the stated physical interpretation. Fits within
restricted matrix classes demonstrate dependence on the chosen admissible class and
basis.

The reusable composition preserves these distinctions:

- calculation produces the declared decomposition result;
- verification checks the stated algebra through an independent route;
- visualization presents calculated and verified content; and
- the correlated result retains those outputs without promoting their evidence class.

The operation does not infer a physical potential from a Hamiltonian alone. It does not
choose the state space, basis, energy reference, unit convention, kinetic reference,
model class, or fit metric on behalf of a caller. It does not establish semiconductor
or material-model validation.

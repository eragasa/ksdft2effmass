# Scientific interpretation

QHO1D figures compare finite spatial truncations with declared analytical controls on
explicit common coordinates. They distinguish state-shape discrepancy, ordered-energy
discrepancy, Gram defects, and residual diagnostics rather than pooling them into one
error claim.

Finite box and grid sweeps do not establish continuum convergence or uncertainty.
Eigenstate agreement is subject to the declared ordering and alignment conventions.
Small Gram or residual defects establish only their finite represented checks. The
figures do not establish physical validation, material transferability, publication
acceptance, or evidence sealing.

Moving presentation classes into a package changes no scientific value or evidence
status.

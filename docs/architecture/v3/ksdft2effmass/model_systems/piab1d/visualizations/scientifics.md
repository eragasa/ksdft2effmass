# Scientific interpretation

The PIAB1D figures present one finite nondimensional particle-in-a-box analysis and its
recorded controls. They keep continuum analytical values, finite-difference values,
eigensolver residuals, boundary realization, reduction residuals, convergence series,
selection, and decomposition diagnostics visually distinct.

A rendered trend does not establish an asymptotic convergence theorem. A small residual
does not establish model adequacy. Retained-dimension selection applies only to the
declared finite probability criterion. Kinetic-potential plots show the recorded finite
decomposition and model classes; they do not establish unique physical
identifiability.

The thirteen-PNG inventory remains authenticated provisional or retained presentation
according to its execution owner. Moving source modules supplies no new scientific
evidence and does not alter the evidence status of existing figures.

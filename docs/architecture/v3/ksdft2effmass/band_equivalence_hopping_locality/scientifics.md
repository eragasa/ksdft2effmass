# Scientific interpretation

## Scientific question

The controlled question is whether complete rank-two Hamiltonian fields related by a
declared momentum-dependent unitary transformation can have identical sampled ordered
bands yet require different finite real-space support to meet the same sampled
Hamiltonian or band-error requirement.

The complete fields are different matrix representations of the same retained spectrum.
The study therefore concerns representation-dependent locality and finite-support model
adequacy, not physical inequivalence of the untruncated complete Hamiltonians.

## Family interpretation

The four family identities are independent geometric replications:

- orthogonal periodic-2D square;
- nonorthogonal periodic-2D graphene-inspired;
- orthogonal periodic-3D simple cubic; and
- nonorthogonal periodic-3D silicon-inspired.

They share one typed nominal source owner where the mathematics is common, but they do
not share calculation identity, geometry, thresholds, execution records, evidence
status, or conclusions. “Graphene-inspired” and “silicon-inspired” describe synthetic
lattice geometry only; these calculations are not graphene or silicon material models.

For normalized sample weights \(w_q\), same-space retained rank-\(r\) Hamiltonians
\(H_q\) and \(\widetilde H_q\), and ordered spectra \(\epsilon_{qn}\) and
\(\widetilde\epsilon_{qn}\), the installed explicit loss semantics are

\[
L_H = \frac{1}{rE_{\mathrm{norm}}^2}\sum_q w_q
\lVert H_q-\widetilde H_q\rVert_F^2,
\qquad
L_\epsilon = \frac{1}{rE_{\mathrm{norm}}^2}\sum_q w_q
\sum_{n=1}^r(\epsilon_{qn}-\widetilde\epsilon_{qn})^2.
\]

For \(r=2\), the factor \(1/r\) is exactly the Ragasa2026a per-state factor. For
higher rank, it is the cross-rank quadratic normalization selected by the prospective
Ragasa2026c protocol. `norm_energy_scale` declares the arbitrary positive
normalization magnitude \(E_{\mathrm{norm}}>0\). It is neither an energy origin, a
signed energy datum, nor a band gap, and its declared energy-unit identity must match
the compared Hamiltonians. A separate energy-reference identifier declares their common
energy-zero or energy-alignment convention. Retained-space, retained-basis, gauge,
geometry, and reciprocal-sample-inventory identities are also mandatory parts of the
comparison definition. Operator loss includes every retained matrix element. Spectral
loss compares ordered eigenvalues. The declared rank, normalization, identities, sample
shape, and exactly normalized binary64 sample weights are part of the scientific value;
normalized RMS channels alone do not carry their semantic identity. These identifiers
are explicit caller declarations: the loss analyzers do not infer physical state-space,
gauge, geometry, sample-inventory, unit, or energy-zero truth from bare numeric arrays.
Binary64 overflow, underflow, and nonfinite arithmetic at squared terms, weighted terms,
finite sums, denominators, and normalized results are reported as numerical failures
rather than clipped. This rank-general loss owner does not by itself install the open
periodic-2D/3D rank-\(N\) parent, alignment, mesh, or evidence protocols.

Installed direct lifecycles compose the square, triangular/honeycomb, simple-cubic, and
FCC/diamond families independently. Every stage set except the square case is installed
without a retained execution. The nonorthogonal periodic-2D composition keeps its
matched-square and triangular Bravais controls distinct from its honeycomb-basis case
and does not represent graphene. The nonorthogonal periodic-3D composition likewise
keeps its matched-simple-cubic and FCC Bravais controls distinct from its diamond-basis
case and does not represent silicon. Support for a family identity in a typed request—or
the existence of direct scripts—is not evidence that its calculation has been executed,
verified, retained, sealed, or accepted.

- parent Hermiticity, eigensystem, projector, and retained-reconstruction checks;
- external-gap and seed-overlap qualifications;
- frame transport, rank, closure, and plaquette diagnostics when applicable;
- direct sampled band equivalence and represented-field covariance;
- complete Fourier reconstruction, Parseval, Hermiticity, and Nyquist channels;
- reference/comparison hopping power and excess nonzero-hopping weight;

- minimum tolerance-satisfying sampled shell-support decisions; and
- amplitude midpoint and analytic flat-parent resolution diagnostics.

These quantities have meaning only with their recorded basis, gauge normalization,
finite meshes, support metric, and applicability state.

The installed hopping-power channel is cell-block locality. It answers how total
Frobenius power is distributed across integer cell displacements and metric shells. It
does not answer which matrix-element, orbital, or MLWF pair carries that power. Optional
basis offsets contribute only geometric range diagnostics. The peer orbital- and
MLWF-resolved meanings are prospective contracts under
[`ksdft2effmass.hopping_locality`](../hopping_locality/index.md).

## Nonclaims

Neither the typed lifecycle nor a passing verification establishes:

- a continuum limit or convergence rate;
- uncertainty quantification or error bars;
- a material Hamiltonian or material validation;
- a universally optimal gauge or localization method;
- Wannier90 spread minimization;
- a universal relationship between spectral equivalence and locality;
- transferability of sampled tolerances to another family;
- successful Quantum ESPRESSO or Wannier90 execution;
- retained-evidence admission, sealing, publication acceptance, or release.

SHA-256 establishes byte identity and integrity only. Canonical typed reconstruction
establishes record admission only. Software tests establish software behavior only.
Independent numerical agreement establishes only the declared finite comparisons.

calculation result remains its own scientific owner. The only cross-result owner is a
named refinement assessment that records adjacent-mesh changes and explicit unresolved
dispositions without embedding or replacing the source results. Half-step tensor meshes
do not provide the periodic-1D shared-node comparison, and no periodic-1D tolerance,

positive normalization energy scale. A prospective controlled-synthetic comparison
may declare `synthetic-dimensionless-energy-v1` with \(E_{\mathrm{norm}}=1\), but that
identity is not a material energy, gap estimate, or acceptance threshold. Complete
physical-metric shells remain indivisible.

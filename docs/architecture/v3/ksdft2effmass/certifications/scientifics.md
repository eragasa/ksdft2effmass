# Certification scientific interpretation

A certification answers one explicitly encoded proposition. It does not expand the
claim beyond that proposition.

For the current periodic-1D rank-one leaf, an accepted result would establish only the
finite first joint passage of the two declared total-maximum error objectives on the
frozen construction/evaluation inventories, provided the outward-enclosure containment
claims and their independent verification are sound. It would not establish:

- continuum or off-mesh error bounds;
- material validity or transfer to silicon;
- scientific adequacy of the parent model;
- rank-two or higher-rank behavior;
- optimal localization or support beyond the closed inventory;
- uncertainty quantification; or
- publication or human acceptance.

A rejected result is a bounded rejection of that exact proposition. An unresolved result
must remain unresolved; it cannot be converted to acceptance by midpoint inspection,
binary64 agreement, or a looser unreviewed precision or threshold.

The checked retained runtime-certificate count is currently zero. The optional local Arb
parent and mediated enclosers establish only a partial producer capability. No direct
objective or independent containment verifier is implemented, and the tests do not
create retained scientific evidence or alter the scientific, validation, or
human-acceptance boundaries above.

# Scientific contract

The record asserts correlation, not scientific validity. Its components must share external-gap, transport, frame, harmonic, and parent-hopping controls, while mesh and amplitude inventories may differ according to their declared roles. The family template must represent the full parent.

Parent-family variation, momentum refinement, amplitude refinement, qualification outcomes, and truncation consequences remain distinct. The record neither adds error channels nor interprets passing software verification as convergence or validation.

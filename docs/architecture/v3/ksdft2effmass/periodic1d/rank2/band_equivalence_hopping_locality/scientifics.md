# Scientific interpretation

The flat control isolates a representation effect: a momentum-dependent basis rotation
preserves the two ordered eigenvalues pointwise while redistributing represented
Hamiltonian weight across finite cell displacements. A single global real rotation is a
strictly smaller admissible alignment class than the generating momentum-dependent
rotation, so its residual can remain nonzero despite exact pointwise band equivalence.

The flat control does not establish retained-subspace isolation, frame-transport
qualification, robustness across parent families, or continuum convergence.
Negative-Nyquist conversion, interpolation, and deliberate truncation are
finite-representation choices, not physical-model error. Likewise, exact result-byte
admission and SHA-256 identity establish integrity of the supplied representation, not
scientific validity.

The separate embedded-subspace/rank2 core tests the same representation mechanism only after its own
sampled external-gap and closed-loop transport gates pass. Those are finite-mesh
qualification statements, not continuous-Brillouin-zone isolation certificates. A
qualified transport outcome establishes the declared numerical route behavior; it does
not establish convergence. Backend failure, numerical rank failure, and failure of the
stricter scientific singular-value threshold remain different stop states. Independent
canonical-byte verification checks the retained finite result against a second numerical
route; passing it establishes software-route agreement and subject integrity only. It
does not turn the sampled gap into a continuum certificate or establish scientific
validation.

The installed parent-family channel repeats the complete finite calculation and
independent reconstruction at every explicitly declared hopping scale. Agreement across
that bounded inventory is a finite robustness observation only; it is not an exhaustive
parent-model claim and does not establish continuum convergence.

The embedded truncation channel is a finite representation-consequence study after
qualification. Negative-Nyquist removal, ordinary omitted power, interpolation error,
deliberate truncation error, and total error are separate channels and are not asserted
to be additive. Hamiltonian and sorted-spectrum maxima are independently evaluated.
Passing its in-memory independent comparison establishes finite software-route agreement
only; it does not authenticate retained bytes, choose a physically adequate cutoff, or
establish mesh or continuum convergence.

The installed momentum-refinement channel has its own typed request, separate thresholds,
and closed qualification-stopped, converged, and unresolved outcomes. Its convergence
label means only that the required final finite doubling comparisons satisfy those
thresholds. It is not a proof of a continuum limit, and an unresolved outcome is not a
backend, rank, external-gap, or scientific transport-threshold failure. The independent
in-memory comparison establishes software-route agreement for that refinement value; it
does not authenticate retained refinement bytes.

The adaptive-amplitude channel remains separate from momentum refinement. Its converged
variant means only that direct midpoint and disjoint quarter-point interpolation defects
on one fixed finite mesh pass every declared amplitude threshold. Level-cap and
sample-cap outcomes are unresolved algorithmic stops, while a qualification-stopped
outcome retains the parent gate failure instead of presenting interpolation evidence.
Independent reconstruction establishes software-route agreement for this finite adaptive
study; it does not authenticate retained bytes, prove uniform continuum behavior, or
inherit acceptance from momentum refinement, a one-mesh control, or the bounded parent
family.

# Rank-two gauge-alignment scientific interpretation

The selected matrices act from the right within the identified retained two-dimensional
state space. Pointwise alignment and one-global-unitary alignment are different
mathematical constraints and remain separate outputs. Neither changes the physical
parent operator; each changes its finite retained-space representation.

The capability assumes orthonormal frames and the Euclidean $U(2)$ metric. It does not
supply a nonorthogonal metric, generalized adjoint, material interpretation, continuum
convergence claim, or locality conclusion. Those require separately owned models,
representations, and diagnostics.

Because the present transformations are ordinarily unitary,
$U^{-1}=U^\dagger$ and the coordinate-endomorphism basis-change law
$U^{-1}HU$ agrees numerically with the compressed sesquilinear-form law
$U^\dagger HU$. A future metric-aware contract must choose which represented object and
adjoint it means before defining operator transformation or alignment objectives; the
present capability does not silently choose between those inequivalent generalizations.

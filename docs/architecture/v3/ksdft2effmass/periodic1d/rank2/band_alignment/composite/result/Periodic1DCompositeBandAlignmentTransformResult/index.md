# `Periodic1DCompositeBandAlignmentTransformResult`

This record owns one named complete transform for the reference, attacked, or pointwise channel. It retains the centered cyclic representatives and two-by-two complex hopping blocks, the ordinary-lattice representatives after removing the negative Nyquist class, normalized cyclic-conversion power, construction reconstruction and cyclic-Hermiticity diagnostics, and disjoint-mesh interpolation errors in normalized Hamiltonian and sorted-band channels. Channel names form a closed ordered inventory.

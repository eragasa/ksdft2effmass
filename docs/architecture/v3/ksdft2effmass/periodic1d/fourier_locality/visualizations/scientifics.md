# Visualization scientific interpretation

Figures communicate authenticated finite values; they do not create new evidence or
scientific claims.

The locality figure labels the first-to-second gap and its minimum as sampled, compares
evaluation and production construction inventories, displays the complete cyclic
coefficient decay, separates interpolation from adjacent-mesh refinement, keeps
truncation and mediated/direct totals distinct, exposes omitted coefficient norms, marks
only attained nondominated/near-tie supports, and shows every mesh's first and sustained
threshold passage. Connected finite points are not an inferred continuum frontier.

The route-agreement figure distinguishes mediated and direct total errors from their
coefficient-vector and construction-sample defects. Visual proximity is not substituted
for an independently verified numerical comparison.

No figure may imply global isolation, continuum convergence, uncertainty, an optimized
rank-one gauge, material applicability, Formal certification, sealing, or publication
acceptance.

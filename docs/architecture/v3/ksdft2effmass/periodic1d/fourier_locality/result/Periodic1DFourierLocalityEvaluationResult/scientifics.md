# `Periodic1DFourierLocalityEvaluationResult` scientific interpretation

These values are finite samples of the two lowest ordered eigenvalue fields of the exact
authored four-state parent. The section does not claim an isolated continuum band.

The evaluation grid is disjoint from every construction grid in the authored
configuration. It therefore supports a withheld finite diagnostic rather than reuse of
training samples. Disjoint sampling does not produce a statistical uncertainty model or
a global approximation bound.

The adjacent-minus-lowest difference contributes to the bounded sampled-gap diagnostic.
Its positivity on the finite inventory is not a global isolation certificate.

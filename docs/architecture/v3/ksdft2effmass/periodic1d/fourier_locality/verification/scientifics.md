# Verification scientific interpretation

Verification asks whether the admitted bytes encode the same declared finite result as
an independent implementation and whether the authenticated visualization projection is
complete and correlated.

A passing aggregate supports:

- byte identity of the admitted subject;
- strict typed-wire validity;
- bounded agreement of two finite binary64 routes; and
- exact projection correlation.

It does not support global band isolation, continuum convergence, uncertainty bounds,
physical material validity, Formal theorem checking, evidence sealing, publication
acceptance, or release.

A failed scientific gate and a failed producer/verifier comparison are different. The
former may be a valid calculated outcome; the latter means the result has not passed
independent verification.

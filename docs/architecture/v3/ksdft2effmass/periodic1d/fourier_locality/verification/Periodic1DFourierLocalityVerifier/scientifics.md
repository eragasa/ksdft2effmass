# `Periodic1DFourierLocalityVerifier` scientific interpretation

This class is an independent finite-calculation verifier, not an alternative scientific
experiment.

Its independence protects against production-composition, transform, fit, diagnostic,
and decision defects. Shared record constructors protect the one declared wire rather
than weakening independence.

Passing verification means the admitted result is consistently reconstructed under the
declared finite model and tolerances. It does not prove the model's physical relevance,
a global spectral gap, continuum convergence, or error uncertainty.

The verifier may correctly authenticate a result whose sampled qualification failed.
Rendering policy may then choose to show the authenticated failure, but it must not
present that result as a passing scientific conclusion.

# `Periodic1DFourierLocalityVisualizationData` numerics

Projection construction selects existing typed quantities and dispositions by exact
field identity. It may calculate presentation magnitudes such as `abs(t_R)` and explicit
normalizations by recorded positive `E_ref`; it may not calculate a new diagnostic or
decision.

All coordinate and series lengths are constructor-correlated. Per-mesh series preserve
the configured mesh order. Per-support series preserve `R=0,...,15`. Boolean masks have
the same extent as their objective points. The first-passage marker is absent exactly
when the authenticated identifier is `None`.

Canonical typed serialization preserves units, complex source values where required,
array shapes, plot inventory, and source identity. The verifier compares this complete
projection before recording its digest.

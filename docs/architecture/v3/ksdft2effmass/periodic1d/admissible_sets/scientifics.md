# Scientific scope

## Established by one passing result

For the exact finite synthetic parent, meshes, candidate rectangle, nine-angle family,
loss normalization, thresholds, and numerical resolution stored in that result, the
calculation may retain:

- one constructive common witness for the compatible threshold case;
- one bounded separation certificate, or an explicit unresolved outcome, for the second
  threshold case;
- evaluation-only spectral and operator diagnostics at the retained proof points; and
- distinct cyclic-conversion and ordinary-tail locality diagnostics.

The result is conditional on its exact authenticated composite baseline and cannot be
transferred to another parent, mesh, gauge family, energy reference, threshold policy,
or parameter domain without a new result.

## Not established

A passing software verification does not establish continuum convergence, material
validity, Wannier localization, an optimal gauge, uncertainty quantification, robustness
to prospective threshold selection, scientific acceptance, or publication readiness.
The finite global real rotations are the declared comparison family, not all admissible
gauges. Evaluation values do not convert training-selected thresholds into independent
validation.

The binary64 separation certificate is not a Formal proof. No exact rational or outward
interval certificate export and checker result is admitted. SHA-256 authenticates bytes,
not scientific truth.

Historical retained M3 values and post-hoc sensitivity outputs are provenance-only for
this runtime. They are neither silently reclassified as successor results nor rewritten
under the canonical typed wire.

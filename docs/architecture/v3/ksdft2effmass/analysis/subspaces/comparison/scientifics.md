# Scientific interpretation

The projector difference measures disagreement between two finite retained subspaces,
not disagreement between arbitrarily signed eigenvector columns.

For pristine PIAB1D, sampled sine and numerical spectral subspaces should coincide in
exact arithmetic. For QHO1D, the sampled-Hermite trial subspace and finite-grid
spectral subspace generally differ.

A small projector difference does not establish equality of retained operator
coordinates, continuum convergence, basis completeness, or physical model adequacy.
Unequal retained dimensions require a separately specified comparison and are rejected
by this contract.

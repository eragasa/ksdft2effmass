# `AuthorSuppliedPublisherAbstractEvidence`

`AuthorSuppliedPublisherAbstractEvidence` binds one explicitly authorized APS abstract
URL, DOI, title, authors, publication date, abstract text, exact source-page SHA-256,
citation-key disposition, and any source or extraction warnings. Its provenance is
always `AUTHOR_SUPPLIED_AD_HOC` and scope always `PUBLISHER_ABSTRACT`.

Only active accepted evidence may carry a canonical citekey. Candidate evidence may
carry only a proposed noncanonical citekey. The record makes no full-paper, rights,
scientific-validation, publication, or human-acceptance claim.

## Shadow replay and cutover

Historical shadow replay compared retained legacy and local observations by
status, issue code, paths, related identities, ordering, inventories, exit
status, and structured output. Differences were classified as equivalent,
intentional, deferred, or defect; deferred and defect observations blocked
cutover.

The route owner is `harness/local/validation-route.json`. Route selection and
rollback actions are pure over explicit configuration; operational changes to
the route file remain separate actions. Historical checksum catalogs and replay
reports preserve the conditions they observed and are not rewritten to match the
current tree.

records. Current maintained files use their current canonical identities.
Renames preserve an explicit old-to-new path or node mapping and do not turn an
unchanged requirement into new evidence.
